Shared password · read-only

Friends explore approved research

  • Six plain-language method pages
  • Evidence badges and comparison views
  • Hover terms and full glossary

  • Separate invention and Egypt annex

No live AI and no raw repo mirror.

Correction loop

A reader can flag confusion or an error, but the correction returns to the research workspace. It is reviewed and republished through the same approval boundary rather than editing scientific content in place.
