Every score opens into

Rating + evidence card

  • Criterion-specific ordinal rating
  • Plain-language label and rationale
  • Exact rubric version
  • Source-supported, inferred, AI-assessed, or unknown basis
  • Claim/source links and unresolved counterevidence
  • Human reviewer and review date

Unknown = no rating. It is not zero and sorts last unless the reader asks to find the largest gaps.

What still needs humans?

Each method lists the next unresolved work by type:

source retrieval · analytical-method work · controlled laboratory evidence · pilot evidence · independent product/use testing · treatment acceptance · regulatory review

These are evidence gaps, not experiment authorization or operating instructions.

Sorting rules

  • Sort one criterion at a time; no default aggregate.
  • Show the evidence-basis label beside the score.
  • Unknown sorts last, or first in “find missing evidence” mode.
  • Filters can restrict results to source-supported assessments.
  • Scores never override the detailed gaps or dissent.
