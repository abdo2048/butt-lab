Final scope: foundation plus one cigarette-butt pilot

Preserve the research system; add a small approved-publication layer rather than reorganizing the whole repository.

Phase 1

Content contract

Define the minimum records, evidence states, glossary terms, source rights, validators, and export allowlist.

Phase 2

Batch-02 pilot content

Prepare six method families, comparison facts, sources, glossary, Egypt annex, and separate invention.

Phase 3

Reader app

Build the plain-language pages, evidence views, search, tooltips, source links, and secondary audit drawers.

Phase 4

Public release

Run scientific, rights, exposure, accessibility, responsive, and link checks; require operator approval; deploy static output.

Pilot deliverables
  • Start here: what a butt contains and how to read the evidence.
  • Six method explanations: mechanical, thermal, chemical, biological, incorporation, and product routes.
  • Compare and sort: separate criterion scores for evidence, ease, material fate, product readiness, and validation work, each with its basis.
  • Source library: citation, locator, original link, approved PDF only where allowed.
  • Glossary: reusable terms powering page tooltips.
  • Separate destinations: new unvalidated invention, Egypt annex, and how the research was made.

Migration rule

Do not rename history into readability

Existing batch files, workflow state, panel reports, and IDs remain intact as audit records.

The publication layer assigns clear reader titles and links back to those records. It does not rewrite or duplicate the scientific truth.

Only batch-02 content is normalized first. A second recyclable material should reuse the same records and views before the model is generalized further.

Pilot succeeds when

  • A non-specialist can explain the six approaches and why evidence differs.
  • Every claim can reach a source locator or an explicit unresolved state.
  • AI additions and the invention are impossible to mistake for source facts.
  • The public bundle contains no private/raw workflow files or unauthorized PDFs.
  • Updating one approved record updates every generated view consistently.
  • The same foundation can host a second material without a new architecture.

Explicit non-goals

  • No live AI, autonomous publishing, CMS, database, or moderation queue.
  • No universal recycling ontology or migration of every historical file.
  • No opaque aggregate “best method” verdict, safety verdict, or claim that an invention is validated.
  • No graph as the primary navigation and no stock Quartz dependency.
  • No Arabic content in the MVP, only an RTL/localization-safe foundation.
  • No experimental execution or authorization.
