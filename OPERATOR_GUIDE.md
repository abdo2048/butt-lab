# Operator guide (reviewed 2026-11-04)

## Route and permanent message
1 Harvester -> 2 Deep-dive -> STOP for 3 Red-team fusion -> STOP for 4 Inventor fusion -> 5 Synthesizer. AUTO continues until the next manual gate or real resource limit; saved checkpoints let another AI resume.

> Read START_HERE.md and resume the workflow in AUTO mode.

Give repo access, or attach START_HERE.md, AGENTS.md, charter.md, WORKFLOW.md, state/workflow.yaml and needed inputs. No Computer/Council credits, paid API, local inference or router required by the protocol. Actual service limits remain. Transfer approved outputs/checkpoint before changing AIs; unsaved work is not accessible to the next one. Do not upload credentials/private material to public chats.

## Stages 1 and 2
Start AUTO. It reuses old research, covers companies including Code Effort/TerraCycle, completes the scoped landscape and selected reconstructions, then freezes a Stage-3 packet. Four successive AIs can continue the same logical worker. Check current_stage, status, checkpoint, next_action and pending_manual_action in state/workflow.yaml. It must not claim all methods worldwide found or invent secret recipes to close gaps.
When Stage 2 is complete_scoped and Stage 3 awaiting_manual, ensure packet/files are saved. Default AUTO will stop there, not launch other models.

## Suggested hosted panel, no API setup
A: ChatGPT Free. B: Gemini web without paid AI plan. C: Arena Direct with an explicitly selected third family if currently offered, preferably not the same OpenAI/Google families. Judge/clerk: ordinary Perplexity chat with available tools, or another capable named chat; Council/Computer unnecessary. Check account/tool limits and current availability; no fixed quota or version is promised.

Arena Direct/Side-by-Side offers model selection. Side-by-Side can give two raw reports; get the third separately. Agent Mode is useful for research/GitHub, but hidden orchestrators/fallbacks mean new Agent sessions do not guarantee different models. Avoid anonymous Battle for named-family independence. If identities are unknown, say so; diversity/reduced-panel exceptions require your explicit decision. Do not assume normal chats can read/write your repo: attach the packet and import Markdown.

## Stage 3: your checklist
1 Freeze a saved packet version/commit and choose services/models.
2 Send the fixed PANEL command from FUSION_PROTOCOL.md to separate sessions for A/B/C plus the SAME packet. Do not include peer answers. Assign explicit slots for simultaneous runs.
3 Save raw worker-a/b/c.md under panels/batch-01/stage-3/round-01/. A clerk may import them without rewriting. Do not merge unreviewed shared-file edits.
4 Give a fresh judge the JUDGE command, common packet and three reports. Save judge.md.
5 Read the disagreements/evidence; explicitly accept, request a bounded revision, or reject. Clerk checks files/versions and records your actual statement. Commit approval alone is not scientific acceptance.
6 Resume AUTO. An accepted Stage 3 leads to a Stage-4 packet and another STOP; unresolved slots/gates are reported precisely, not hidden.

## Stage 4 and Stage 5
Repeat A/B/C/JUDGE with stage=4. Inventors get evidence, accepted critique, gaps, target requirements and prior H1-H3 as seeds. Initial proposals remain separate; judge compares/combines with attribution and adversarial screening. Explicitly accept/revise/reject its portfolio. Do not confuse consensus with clean material.
After accepted Stage 4 with no blocking decision, use the same AUTO message. One Synthesizer finishes Stage 5 or checkpoints if interrupted. Final status desk_complete means research package complete, not empirically proven or approved for consumer use.

## Interruptions
Keep stage/round/slot/packet IDs when resuming; retain partial raw outputs/provenance. Material input changes require a new packet/round. Default two revision rounds before another operator decision. If no tools can save, export Markdown plus checkpoint patch. The files cannot start a chat, supply capacity or guarantee background persistence.
