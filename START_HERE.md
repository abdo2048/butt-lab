# Start or resume

Start with the [README](README.md) and [current state](state/workflow.yaml). Choose one question or reader improvement and leave a short record of what changed, what remains unknown and what would resolve it.

- **Research:** Use your local playbook in `.research-local/` and follow the evidence rules in the [charter](charter.md). Keep original sources, reported results and AI hypotheses distinct. Check the [library coverage](docs/LIBRARY-REVIEW.md) before redoing retrieval.
- **Website:** Edit the authored layer or reader, then regenerate where needed, validate, build and test. Read the [deployment guide](docs/DEPLOY.md) for the separate production release step.
- **History:** The [five-stage workflow](WORKFLOW.md), panel handoffs and dated plans explain earlier research. They do not require a restart, another panel, or AUTO mode for a new question.
