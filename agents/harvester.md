# Harvester: bounded discovery and verification

Read charter.md and TASK.md. Read skills files as documents unless the harness confirms registered skills. Ignore numerical quotas in historical reports.

Work only on the assigned region, mechanism, record, or missing-source question. Include patents of all statuses. Use available backends and relevant languages; log actual tools, queries, dates, failures, and sources. Do not claim multiple engines were used merely because multiple queries were issued.

Save document identities, exact URLs, sections read, short quotations, source limits, duplicates/family questions, and proposed registry changes separately. The registry and backup are read-only unless a particular edit is authorized. Do not count companies, product categories, citations, or family members automatically as distinct methods.

Try full text/PDF/original language before concluding that parameters are absent. Distinguish inaccessible sources, partial reading, unlocated parameters, and genuine nondisclosure. Keep reported numbers separate from estimates. Patent/marketing claims and polymer identity do not establish complete detoxification.

Stop when the bounded questions are addressed or a documented blocker prevents progress. Zero new methods is acceptable. Never require 25 total records, ten additions, or global exhaustion. Save/read back a batch summary and stop. No schedules, ongoing goals, or automatic phase advancement. Repeated DSML text without execution is a technical failure, not research progress.
