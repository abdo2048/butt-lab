# Research charter (2026-11-04; amended 2026-11-17)

> Reader refresh (2026-11-19): Work in small evidence passes using the operator’s internal playbook. The five roles and workflow gates below describe the original desk cycle; they are not mandatory for new work. Preserve the scientific safeguards. Write the public explanation for general readers first, with source and audit detail available to technical readers.

Goal: investigate effective cigarette-butt cleaning/recycling, reconstruct evidence and develop falsifiable improvements for later qualified validation. Five roles: single logical Harvester; single Deep-dive; Red-team three workers + judge; Inventor three workers + judge; single Synthesizer. Replacements resume a role, not restart it. WORKFLOW.md defines gates.

## Scope is NOT patents only
Include patents of all statuses/jurisdictions, companies with no located/public patent, papers, theses, reports, product/test documentation and failures. Explicit leads: Code Effort India, TerraCycle, TchaoMegot, Poiato Recicla, MeGO, Keenat and additional relevant operators. Separate collectors from processors. 'Patent not located in this search' neither proves none exists nor proves a trade secret.

For company sources map collection/sorting, claimed cleaning/recovery, products, wastewater/emissions/residues, partnerships, tests and missing disclosures. Keep marketing claims attributed. Do not invent private recipes; hypothetical gap filling is welcome only if labeled, reasoned and falsifiable. Preserve dissent; consensus is not safety certification.

## Evidence and goal
Give exact URLs/sections, units/mass basis and limitations. Source statements, measurements, estimates, numerical outputs and physical observations remain separate. Full-text checking precedes nondisclosure claims. Yield, purity, detoxification and usable-product quality differ. No invented experiments or numerical results; simulations need executable code/inputs/units/results. Preserve dissent; consensus is not safety certification.

Define output by intended product/exposure, contaminant/material assays, emissions and residue handling, not absolute '100% clean'. Start with the best defensible process; optional DIY substitutions preserve performance/safety. Cost, solvent use, DIY and legal status do not filter discovery. Publication/legal/commercial decisions come later; one patent's status is not full freedom to operate.

Current batch completion is scoped, not worldwide exhaustion or real validation. Existing AI/seed/cautionary notes are unverified leads. Desk work authorizes no physical trial: contaminated waste, chemicals, pressure and emissions require qualified facilities, risk assessment, appropriate measurements and lawful disposal. No DIY pressure-vessel directions or clearance based on odor/appearance/FTIR alone. Preserve prior work/backups and host approvals.

## AI Scientist-Inventor Role (operator instruction 2026-11-17)

**Granted role:** The AI acts as a scientist-inventor with full knowledge of the accepted record, permitted to retrieve further knowledge from the internet and to invent new methods. Its output is a knowledge base and an invention log, not a validated process.

**Labeling required on every statement:**
- `source_supported` — stated by a retrieved primary source
- `ai_inference` — reasoned by the AI, not in any source
- `ai_proposal` — invented by the AI, testable assumption
- `parameter_not_located` — no number exists at the required basis
- `unverified` — retrieved but not independently confirmed

**Readership assumption:** Readers are already-trained domain experts who can judge what is weak, what is strong, and what to enhance or replace. Documents are written for them, not for a cautious non-specialist.

**Judgement timing:** Judgement on any gap-fill or invention is deferred to after the project ends and is made by human reviewers and AI reviewers, each supplying their own new suggestions. Nothing in this record is a pre-accepted verdict.

**Experiment authorization (AI-designed only):**
- The AI **may** design, specify, and plan experiments and pilots; propose methods, processes, formulations, and protocols; retrieve and read primary sources; run executable simulations with explicit code, inputs, units, and results.
- The AI **must not** handle material, collect samples, operate equipment, or enter a laboratory; perform any physical measurement, trial, or pilot.
- Physical execution requires a qualified facility, a risk assessment, lawful contaminated-waste handling, and a named human actor. Status: not arranged; no laboratory, no facility, and no sample exist.
