## How to fill a verdict

- `supported` — the source states the proposition, and the locator points at where.
- `partly supported` — the source states part of it, or states it more narrowly; write what it actually says.
- `unsupported` — the source is readable and does not support the proposition.
- `not located in text` — the extraction does not contain it. This is not `unsupported`: the PDF may still carry it, so the row stays open until someone reads the page.

A verdict is a reading of one source against one sentence. It is not a judgement that the route works, that a material is clean, or that a company's claim is true.

### `shah-2023-review` (Heliyon 2023, 15 strategies, 110 papers reviewed)
- **Corrosion inhibition** (chemical family): nicotine shows 94.6% inhibition in 10% HCl, 91.7% in 15% HCl; with 15% CuCl reaches 97%. *No data on toxicant leaching into solution.*
- **Porous carbon / activated carbon** (thermal/product-routes): 58% carbon in filters; KOH activation at 900°C → 2726 m²/g BET; N-doped carbon for acetylene hydrochlorination (71.8% activity); K₂CO₃ carbon for antibiotic removal (450 mg/g); Ni-carbon for microwave absorption.
- **Biofilm carriers** (biological): CBs as inexpensive carriers in AMBBR/IFAS; porous, rough, high surface area for microbial colonization.
- **Oil sorbents** (product-routes): SiO₂/MTMS/OTS modification → superhydrophobic fibers; 16–26× weight oil absorption; >98% water/oil separation efficiency.
- **Insecticidal** (product-routes): nicotine toxic to *Aedes aegypti/albopictus* larvae, *Plasmodium falciparum*; 2% CB solution reduces survival. *Non-target organism studies needed.*
- **Supercapacitors** (thermal/product-routes): N-doped carbon 330 F/g at 0.5 A/g, 93.5% cycling after 10k cycles; CB-PPy 263 F/g.
- **Li-ion separators** (thermal): electrospun CA/PVDF-HFP membranes; N-doped carbon anodes; carbon-graphene for Li-S.
- **Hot melt adhesives** (product-routes): cellulose diacetate + PVA → biodegradable adhesive with improved shear/peel strength.
- **Estrogen remediation** (chemical): electrospun nanofibers from filters, 2.14 mg/g absorption.
- **Hydrogen storage** (thermal): activated carbon from CBs up to 9.4 wt% H₂; smoked CBs optimal at intermediate activation temp due to metal additives from combustion.
- **Bricks** (material-incorporation): 1% CBs → 8× energy reduction; 2% → porosity 3.96%→20.57%, thermal conductivity 1.08→0.53 W/m·K; compressive strength 25.65→5.22 MPa at 5%. Leachate test mentioned.
- **Acoustic** (product-routes): absorption coefficient 0.8 at 2000 Hz. *Toxicity/heavy metal release not investigated.*
- **CNCs** (chemical): 4-step (ethanolic extraction, bleaching, de-acetylation, H₂SO₄ hydrolysis); 60% Cel-CF, 49% CNC-CF yield. *Hard to remove acetate group and heavy metals without disrupting cellulose.*
- **Cellulose pulp** (chemical): cites Teixeira et al. 2017; dark liquor high COD, toxic nitrogenous compounds.
- **Table 2** lists drawbacks for every strategy: leachate analysis, LCA, toxicity, heavy metal release — mostly "required" or "not investigated".

### `hudina-wo2026074041a2` (WO 2026/074041 A2, Cerdia International, PCT 2025/078253)
- **Mechanical pre-treatment only**: longitudinal slitting + air separation through sieves to remove paper, tobacco, metal heating elements (HT products), adhesives — *explicitly no shredding, no water washing*.
- **Solvent cleaning**: hydrocarbon-based (methanol, ethanol, isopropanol preferred) or water-based; closed-loop distillation recovery of solvent.
- **Contaminants targeted**: nicotine and derivatives, phenols, PAHs — *no mention of heavy metals*.
- **Output**: thermoplastic recyclate (30–40 wt% recycled CA, melt index 2–20 g/10 min); non-woven sandwich structures for air filters/thermal insulation.
- **End-of-Waste claim**: recyclate "nearly equivalent to virgin CA compound" for many applications.

### `teixeira-cellulose-pulp` (Waste Management 60, 2017, 140–150)
- **Alkaline pulping**: 1% NaOH, boiling 3 h, 50 L pilot reactor. Four steps: dissolve ash/chemicals, hydrolyze CA→cellulose, remove lignin from tobacco, disaggregate paper.
- **Pulp quality**: tensile index 1.09→11.22 kN·m/kg after cooking; whiteness low (54% vs 85% for pure CA) — needs bleaching.
- **Dark liquor**: COD 29,986 mg/L (BOD/COD=0.3, recalcitrant); nicotine 35.4M ng/mL, NNK 3057, NNN 1635 ng/mL; As 0.04, Cr 0.01 mg/L (Cd, Pb, Ni, Se < LOD) — all below Brazilian discharge limits.
- **Treatment train**: acid precipitation pH 3 (HCl) → 20% COD removal (lignin); chitosan coagulation → 66% COD removal; ozonation → ineffective (sometimes increased COD).
- **Working solution**: reuse clarified effluent (post-acidification) in next pulping cycle — COD stabilizes after 5 cycles (≈60,000 mg/L). *No liquid effluent discharge.*
- **Licensed to Poiato Recicla (Brazil)**, pilot plant March 2016, processing tons, selling pulp to cardboard factory.

### `recig-autorizzazione-impianto` (Provincial AUT, Trento, 2025)
- **Process**: sieving (R13) → wet paper separation → washing at 40–60°C with water only (no detergents/acids/bases) → drying at ~80°C, 2–5 h → thermal mixing (Lico machine) at 200–210°C with up to 10% Triacetin → granulation.
- **Product**: CA conforming to UNIPLAST-UNI 10667 (R3 End of Waste), 100 t/yr capacity.
- **Emissions**: E1 (2500 Nm³/h, activated carbon for dust); E2 (4500 Nm³/h, bag + carbon filters for dust + COT). COT limit 50 mg/Nm³; VOCs, acetone monitored.
- **Wastewater**: washing water collected, sent to authorized treatment plants. *No on-site treatment described.*
- **Metals**: permit Annex 1 Table 2 criteria do *not* include Cd, Pb, As, Hg (the Butt Lab four metals).
