# Keenat LCA Retreat (mandatory red-team benchmark)

Keenat (Bordeaux, FR) patented a dry thermal depollution (no water, no
solvent, ~200 C stages), built a treatment site, produced plastic plates —
then their 2022 life-cycle assessment concluded that recycling butts into
new plastic objects was NOT environmentally beneficial. They closed the site
and pivoted to solid recovered fuel (CSS) for cement kilns. They had also
R&D-tested supercritical CO2 and not commercialized it.

Sources: Le Parisien 2026-10-15; keenat.com (recyclage-valorisation-megots);
Sud Ouest 2026-09-03.

## Use in this lab
Any spec claiming environmental benefit must include an LCA-style accounting:
energy in, transport, chemical inputs, waste streams, displacement of virgin
material. A method that only moves toxins around or costs more energy than it
saves must be marked as failing this benchmark.
