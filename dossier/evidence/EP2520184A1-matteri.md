## Short supporting quotations
- Abstract/description: process “consisting in: a step (10) of removing at least said residual tobacco (1 c) from the butt (1) and a step (30) of washing the butt (1), at least partly removing the harmful substances from the butt (1) deprived of the residual tobacco (1 c).”
- Claim 1: same two steps: removing residual tobacco; washing the tobacco-deprived butt to at least partly remove harmful substances.
- Claim 2: washing step contemplates use of a detergent.
- Claim 3: “said detergent consists of 5% sodium hypochlorite.”
- Claim 5: optional rinsing step (40) after washing to remove possible residual substances.
- Claim 6: rinsing fluid includes at least hydrogen peroxide; claims 7–8 add sweet-smelling oils/dyes.
- Description advantage: “the liquid from washing 30, in particular when washing is carried out with water alone … contains nicotene and can therefore be utilised for eliminating deposits and oxidation from pipelines for oil and gas.”
- Description advantage: at end of process, butt “can be reused for manufacturing handkerchiefs and the like.”

## Disclosed mechanism and process parameters
- Mechanism: mechanical removal of residual tobacco from cigarette butt, then washing the butt to remove harmful substances; optional post-wash rinse.
- Parameters disclosed: washing may use detergent; claim 3 specifies detergent = 5% sodium hypochlorite; optional rinse fluid includes at least hydrogen peroxide. Description notes water alone is possible for washing.
- Parameters NOT disclosed: exact washing temperature, contact time, detergent dosing rate beyond the 5% sodium hypochlorite claim, equipment details, cycle time, de-clumping, throughput economics, wastewater treatment beyond the noted reuse of nicotine-containing wash liquid for pipeline deposit/oxidation removal, and verified toxin-removal yields.
