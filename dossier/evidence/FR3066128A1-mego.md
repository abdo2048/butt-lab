## Short supporting quotations (from Google Patents abstract / meta description)
- Characterizing process steps: "(a) first grinding (22) from cigarette butts (40) in a first mill to separate the filter (41) of the organic material (32); b) sieving (23) the filter (41) and the organic material (32) to recover the cellulose acetate (33) contained in the filter; c) washing (24) the filter (41) to separate the cellulose acetate (33) and the paper (34) with water in a wash tank; d) decontamination washing (25) of the cellulose acetate (33) with water in a decontamination tank to obtain the cleaned cellulose acetate (37); e) recycling the water (26) of steps c) and d) by a water recycler; f) pre-cooking drying (27) to obtain dried cellulose acetate (38); g) second milling (28) to obtain fibrous fine particles of cellulose acetate (39)."
- Composition context: butts composed of filter + organic material (ash, paper, tobacco); filter composed of cellulose acetate + paper.

## Disclosed mechanism and process parameters
- Mechanism: mechanical (grinding, sieving, second milling) + water-based washing (wash tank + decontamination tank) + water recycling + drying to obtain clean cellulose acetate fibrous fine particles.
- Parameters disclosed: water as wash medium; separate wash tank and decontamination tank; water recycling loop between steps c/d and e; "pre-cooking drying"; no numeric temperature/time/concentration captured in scraped text.
- NOT disclosed: exact grinding/sieving particle sizes, wash temperatures, contact times, water recycle ratio, drying temperature, CA yield/purity, toxin removal efficiency, equipment details, or economics.
