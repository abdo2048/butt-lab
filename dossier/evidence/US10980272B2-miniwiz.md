## Short supporting quotations
- Abstract: “Disclosed herein are methods and apparatus for recycling cigarette waste, so that the reusable material (e.g., cellulose acetate, CA) in the cigarette waste may be isolated and reused. The cigarette waste is composed of a filter and a non-filter part. The apparatus includes at least, an adjusting unit and a treatment unit, respectively coupled to each other via a conveying member. The adjusting unit is configured to adjust the orientation of each cigarette so that each cigarette is aligned in sequence with the same orientation. The treatment unit is configured to cut out the non-filter part of each cigarette, and physically separating CA from the remaining part of the filter of each cigarette.”
- Claim 1: apparatus comprising conveying member; adjusting unit aligning cigarettes same orientation; treatment unit with holder exposing non-filter part, cutter to cut out non-filter part, pusher to push part of filter out, and clamper to clamp pushed-out filter part so it is physically separated from remaining filter when holder/clamper move apart.
- Claim 2: pushed-out part of filter is substantially made of cellulose acetate (CA).
- Claim 3: optional fluid supply module to supply compressed fluid to conveying member, cutter, and/or holder.
- Claim 6: optional sorting unit sorting cigarettes by size, length, conductivity, or combination.
- Claim 8: remover expels out-of-spec cigarettes via compressed fluid.

## Disclosed mechanism and process parameters
- Mechanism: mechanical/physical separation. Align cigarettes, cut off/remove non-filter part, push a portion of filter out, clamp it, and separate the CA-rich pushed-out filter portion from the remaining filter. Optional compressed-fluid handling and optional sorting by size/length/conductivity.
- Parameters disclosed: none numeric beyond optional compressed fluid and sorting criteria (size/length/conductivity). Claim 2 identifies pushed-out filter part as substantially CA.

## What is NOT disclosed
