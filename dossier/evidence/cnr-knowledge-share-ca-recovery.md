## Short supporting quotations (from scraped page technical features / introduction / advantages)
- Introduction: "Cigarette filters and cigarette butts represent urban waste and contribute to the pollution of water bodies, as they are among the main sources of microplastics. A method is proposed for the disposal of filters and cigarette butts and the recovery of cellulose acetate (CA), to be reintroduced into the production cycle as a secondary raw material. The proposed method does not provide for prior separation of the components (paper, tobacco residues and ash) of cigarette butts or pre-treatment."
- Technical features (steps): "(a) soaking of filters and cigarette butts, in solvent (acetone) to solubilise AC and nicotine; (b) removal of paper, ash and tobacco residue by filtration; (c) addition of anti-solvent (water) to the solution to induce precipitation of AC, which can then be recovered/filtered; (d) recovery for a new acetone treatment cycle from the residual mixture by boiling; (e) treatment (using known techniques) or start-up for disposal in a suitable plant of the waste produced, consisting of the pollutants concentrated in a small quantity of water. The AC solid obtained is clean (the pollutants remain in the acetone and water mixture) and can be reused to make various objects (e.g. spectacle frames, masks, shields, strings, ...)"
- Advantages: "Simple process, implemented with standard techniques; Cheap process; Low environmental impact process (small quantities of water and concentrated nicotine as process waste, sent for treatment)."
- Possible applications: Urban Waste Management; Plastics Industry (producers of cellulose acetate); Tobacco Industry; Eco-design Industry.

## Disclosed mechanism and process parameters
- Mechanism: solvent extraction of cellulose acetate and nicotine from butts using acetone; filtration to remove paper/ash/tobacco; water anti-solvent precipitation of CA; acetone recovery by boiling; waste treatment/disposal of concentrated pollutant water.
- Parameters disclosed: solvent = acetone; anti-solvent = water; acetone recovery by boiling; no numeric temperatures, times, ratios, or yields.
- NOT disclosed: acetone-to-butt ratio, extraction temperature/time, boiling conditions, CA yield/purity, nicotine concentration in waste, equipment, or economics.
