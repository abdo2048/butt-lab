# Seed Facts (verified, with sources)

## Butt composition
- Smoked butt ~1 g: cellulose acetate (CA) filter ~36%, tobacco fines + paper
  + stems ~54%, ash/moisture ~10%; toxic load (nicotine, PAHs, TSNA, Cd/Pb/As)
  <1% of mass. [VERIFIED-SOURCE: multiple papers, e.g. PMC7659942;
  FR3102180A1 background]
- The toxins are <1% of mass but ~100% of the hazard. Core problem statement.

## TchaoMegot (France) — supercritical CO2
- Patent family: FR3102180A1 (priority 2019-10-22), WO2021079050A1,
  US12065781B2 (granted 2024-08), new US application US20260115774A1 (2025-12).
  Inventor Julien Paque. 6+ international patents.
- Disclosed: contact with supercritical fluid (CO2 preferred) in autoclave;
  pressure preferably 50-350 bar, most preferably 280-320 bar; temperature
  15-80 C (CO2 critical point 31 C / 73.85 bar); co-solvents, chelating agents
  (incl. calixarene) for heavy metals, ultrasound assistance, sieving/grinding
  pre-treatment; contact time claimed 1 min to 8 hours.
  [VERIFIED-SOURCE: patents.google.com/patent/FR3102180A1/en]
- Process built on an EXISTING supercritical CO2 extraction machine (used for
  fish-oil aroma extraction) that was modified; first setup two 5L vessels,
  scale-up to 500L. [VERIFIED-SOURCE: Usine Nouvelle 2021]
- Press quote: ~30 C, "modest" pressure 50/60 bar — CONTRADICTS patent
  preferred range; unresolved, flagged for verification. [UNRESOLVED]
- Outputs: 99.7% clean fiber -> building insulation + textile padding;
  dry toxic concentrate ~0.3% of filter weight -> specialized hazardous lab.
  REACh-certified non-toxic, validated by INERIS.
- SC-CO2 hardware: standard commercial category. Lab 1-5L units $20-50k;
  Chinese 5L $9-20k; used 40L ~$20k; pilot 10-50L $50-120k; new turnkey
  $85-300k. CO2 recovered from industrial emissions, closed loop.

## Keenat / ecomegot (France) — dry thermal
- Patents: FR3110470A1, EP3912739A1 (priority May 2020).
- Method: mechanical separation, successive heat treatments near 200 C
  destroying nicotine, phenol, pesticides; no water, no solvent; fiber ->
  thermo-compressed plastic plates. [VERIFIED-SOURCE: keenat.com, Le Parisien]
- CRITICAL: in 2022 their LCA concluded recycling butts into plastic objects
  was NOT environmentally beneficial; they closed the dedicated site and
  pivoted to solid recovered fuel (CSS) for cement kilns. They also trialed
  supercritical CO2 R&D (odor removal) and did not commercialize it.
  [VERIFIED-SOURCE: Le Parisien 2026-10-15, keenat.com]

## UnB / Poiato Recicla (Brazil) — alkaline pulping
- Technology from Universidade de Brasilia (~2001, Barbosa + Hofmann);
  patent requested 2003, granted 2014 (INPI); licensed exclusively to
  Poiato Recicla (Votorantim, SP, founded 2010, 400M+ butts processed).
- Method: sort -> boil in stainless vat with water + chemicals (alkaline
  pulping per peer-reviewed description), cooking up to 10 h -> filter ->
  press into cellulose pulp -> artisanal paper (~25 butts/sheet).
  [VERIFIED-SOURCE: g1.globo 2019, Waste Management journal S0956053X16305682]
- 20-year term from 2003 filing => LIKELY EXPIRED ~2023. VERIFY on INPI.
  If confirmed dead, method is public domain.

## CNR (Italy) — mask filters
- IT202000003745A1 / WO2021170381A1 / EP3892393A1: crush butts, recover
  cellulosic material -> filters for respiratory masks.

## Cerdia International (Germany) — newest industrial entrant
- WO2026074041A2 (priority 2024-10, published 2026-04): recovering CA from
  post-consumer filters incl. heat-not-burn mouthpieces. Cerdia is a major
  CA tow manufacturer.

## Academic methods (free to build on)
- Green extraction of clean CA: hot water 50 C/60 min -> cold rinses x3 ->
  99% ethanol washes x2. [VERIFIED-SOURCE: PMC7659942]
- Acetic acid as green selective solvent for CA recovery (solubilization-
  desolubilization). [VERIFIED-SOURCE: S0956053X25003265, 2025]
- CA membranes from butts via dissolution routes. [Nature s41598-022-24432-x;
  S0048969724028249]
- Nicotine extractable from tobacco via supercritical CO2 (known technique).

## TerraCycle (US)
- Publishes only outline (shred, sanitize, separate; tobacco/paper composted;
  filters dried, powdered into plastic products). Sanitization chemistry is
  a trade secret. No butt patent found. NOT mineable via patents.

## Failure evidence (see dossier/cautionary/)
- Korea 2021-2023 paving stones from butts: carcinogens found in final product
  despite encapsulation + heat treatment; programs discontinued.
- Keenat LCA retreat (above).
