# Human Operator Validation Protocol

This is YOUR side of the lab: agents simulate, you validate.

SEQUENCE (only after phase 5 shortlist):
1. Read reports/shortlist.md — take the recommended first experiment.
2. Prepare: gloves (nitrile), goggles, ventilation, sealed glass/metal
   containers, labeled hazardous-waste jar for all liquids/solids.
3. Collect butts: fireproof sealed container; extinguish fully.
4. Run the experiment EXACTLY as written in the plan; log everything
   (masses, times, temperatures, observations, photos).
5. Write results to experiments/results/[date]-[experiment].md including
   what DISCONFIRMED expectations. Feed back into the lab as
   [VERIFIED-SOURCE] (you are the primary source now) or refutation.

SAFETY FLOOR (non-negotiable):
- No nicotine-skin contact. No indoor off-gassing without ventilation.
- Never homemade pressure vessels; never >10 bar without certified gear.
- Toxic residues and wash liquids: hazardous waste route, never drain/landfill.
