# Candidate hypotheses 01

2026-11-02. Untested adaptations/hybrids, not claims of patent novelty, freedom to operate, performance or safe SOPs. Basis: reports/process-comparison-01.md and its primary sources. No quantified efficiencies/savings are asserted.

## H1: staged washing with recycle-quality control
Propose counter-current/staged water/ethanol extraction and controlled fiber opening, with separate extraction/waste streams. The paper supports washing; reduced fresh-fluid demand and better access are hypotheses, not measured benefits.
Unknowns: partition/entrainment, contact/loading, polymer/additive loss, recycle buildup and recovery energy.
Failure: polluted medium re-deposits contaminants; opening damages fibers; recovery cost erases savings; residual organics survive.
Falsifier: at the same justified contaminant/material targets, total fresh-fluid/energy/waste burden fails to improve or product contamination increases.
Next: symbolic multistage balances and sensitivity using unknown factors, not assumed removal percentages.

## H2: polymer recovery designed for contaminant rejection
Study dissolution/fractionation/precipitation, washing and recovery as one balance rather than maximizing product mass. CN106928486B supports a recovery lead, not measured rejection of every contaminant.
Unknowns: stream sequence, dry-mass basis/denominator, acid/water composition, polymer integrity, co-precipitation and recycle purity.
Failure: toxins co-precipitate; retained solvent/inorganics inflate yield; washing effluent or polymer damage defeats usefulness.
Falsifier: apparently high recovery cannot meet justified contamination/material targets under complete dry-mass and waste accounting. Conductivity is not a universal neutral-organic assay.
Next: branched source-stream diagram and symbolic polymer/contaminant balance; do not invent missing source values.

## H3: pressurized extraction with conditional polishing
Begin with mechanical preparation and qualified pressurized extraction; add a separate polishing step only if measured residual classes require it. Compare dry-preparation baseline against the hybrid's extra treatment/drying burden.
Support: P describes dense-fluid contact; C claims a recycled neutral-fluid loop. Current recipe and full independent performance remain unresolved.
Unknowns: contaminant-class coverage/metal fate, modifiers, flow access, polymer changes, carryover/losses, compression and disposal burden.
Failure: incomplete removal, dirty recycle, uneven contact or polishing energy/waste dominates. No invented cheap containment substitute.
Falsifier: final/recirculated streams fail predefined balances/quality endpoints or the hybrid loses its claimed benefit under full accounting.
Next: contaminant-class coverage and symbolic recycle/polishing decision thresholds, not a pressure-vessel build recipe.

## Common rule
Compare information value and falsification, not impressive language. No perfect method is established. Future tests require qualified facilities and lawful residue disposal. Define required output performance before optional DIY/open-source component choices; do not reduce output quality to fit convenient equipment.
