## 1. M01-Gate — Dry-Pneumatic + Triboelectric Pre-Fractionation

> **[GAP-CLOSING NOTE — AI, scientist-inventor role]**  
> On a dry-mass basis (`ai_inference`), CA filter + wrapping paper accounts for ~65–75%, residual unburned tobacco ~15–25%, and charred ash/fines ~5–15%. Non-volatile metals (Cd, Pb, As) are inferred to concentrate in ash/fines and residual tobacco, whereas Hg may partition partially into the proximal CA tar/condensate phase.

> **[INVENTION — AI, scientist-inventor role]**  
> **Concept (`ai_proposal`):** Upstream of any wet or solvent process, subject dried, shredded whole butts (<8 wt% moisture) to air-classification (cyclonic winnowing) + triboelectric/electrostatic belt separation to divert ash/fines and residual tobacco as a low-volume, dry, characterized metals stream.  
> **Falsification test (`ai_proposal`):** Falsified if ICP-MS/OES shows **>25% of total Cd, Pb, As, or Hg mass** remains bound to the separated CA tow, or if dry shredding smears tar/ash onto CA fibers.

## 2. `J-H-01-M` — Counter-Current + GAC/IDA Chelation-Polished Wash (F1/J-H-01)

- **Simulation (`ai_inference`):** 100 kg dry-basis butts/cycle, 1,000 L closed-loop wash water, 80 kg dry CA out, 1.5 L/kg wet-CA entrainment (120 L/cycle).
  - **0% purge (entrainment only):** Cycle-20 wash water = 1,076.2 mg/L nicotine (1,614.3 mg/kg CA re-deposited), 0.288 mg/L Cd (0.432 mg/kg CA), 0.346 mg/L Pb.
  - **15% purge/cycle:** Cycle-20 wash water = 517.6 mg/L nicotine (776.3 mg/kg CA re-deposited), 0.139 mg/L Cd (0.208 mg/kg CA), 0.166 mg/L Pb.

> **[INVENTION — AI, scientist-inventor role]**  
> **Concept (`ai_proposal`):** Pair M01-Gate with a 2-stage counter-current wash and inline **granular activated carbon (GAC)** + **iminodiacetic-acid (IDA) chelating resin** polishing on the Stage-1 recycle loop, converting dissolved contaminants into weighable solid spent media.  
> **Falsification test (`ai_proposal`):** Falsified if Cycle-20 CA nicotine >50 mg/kg dry, Cd/Pb exceed use-gate limits, or tar/surfactants foul GAC/IDA beds in <5 cycles.

## 3. `F3-SC` — Microfiltration + Staged Ethanol/CO₂ Antisolvent (F3)

- **Simulation (`ai_inference`):** 100 kg dry CA feed in 1,000 L acetone (784 kg), 92.0 kg CA recovered.
  - **Solvent makeup & energy:** 0.85 kg/kg CA (90% recovery, 2.43 kWh/kg CA) down to 0.13 kg/kg CA (98.5% recovery, 2.66 kWh/kg CA).
  - **Co-precipitation:** Standard water antisolvent leaves 78.3 mg/kg nicotine and 1.90 mg/kg PAHs in CA; staged EtOH/CO₂ wash reduces to 9.8 mg/kg nicotine and 0.22 mg/kg PAHs (`ai_proposal`).

> **[INVENTION — AI, scientist-inventor role]**  
> **Concept (`ai_proposal`):** 1–5 µm PTFE hot-acetone microfiltration (removing insoluble metal salts/ash) + staged ethanol/scCO₂ antisolvent precipitation + pervaporation-assisted solvent recovery.  
> **Falsification test (`ai_proposal`):** Falsified if dope viscosity (>500 cP) blinds 1–5 µm filters, >25% of dissolved Cd/Pb/As/Hg co-precipitates with CA, or residual solvent exceeds 500 mg/kg.

## 4. `F2-VSC` — Vacuum-Vented Extrusion + Acid/S-GAC Off-Gas Capture (F2)

- **Simulation (`ai_inference`):** 100 kg dry butts + 400 kg virgin PP/PE (20 wt% loading, 500 kg composite) at 200 °C.
  - Formulation dilution lowers bulk concentrations 5× on paper (e.g., Cd 1.50 → 0.294 mg/kg; Pb 3.00 → 0.594 mg/kg) while retaining 98–99% of Cd/Pb mass and driving 35% of nicotine (52.5 g) and 60% of Hg (3.0 mg) into off-gas.

> **[INVENTION — AI, scientist-inventor role]**  
> **Concept (`ai_proposal`):** M01-Gate + vacuum-vented twin-screw devolatilization (200 °C, <100 mbar) routed to a chilled acid condenser (pH <3) and sulfur-impregnated GAC (S-GAC) bed.  
> **Falsification test (`ai_proposal`):** Falsified if vacuum extrusion causes >20% tensile-strength loss, residual nicotine >100 mg/kg, or condensate + S-GAC captures <85% of volatilized nicotine/Hg.

## 5. `F5-ED` — Alkaline Deacetylation + BMED NaOH/Acetic Acid Recovery (F5)

- **Simulation (`ai_inference`):** 100 kg dry CA (DS = 2.45) yields **61.2 kg** regenerated cellulose pulp (38.8% mass loss is cleaved acetyl groups), consuming **37.0 kg** stoichiometric NaOH and generating **75.8 kg** sodium acetate (NaOAc) in dark liquor.

> **[INVENTION — AI, scientist-inventor role]**  
> **Concept (`ai_proposal`):** M01-Gate + dark-liquor clarification/GAC polishing + **bipolar-membrane electrodialysis (BMED)** to split NaOAc brine back into recycled NaOH and recoverable acetic acid.  
> **Falsification test (`ai_proposal`):** Falsified if dark-liquor organics cause >25% BMED cell-voltage rise within 10 hours, pulp retains Cd/Pb/As/Hg above contact limits, or BMED energy/membrane cost exceeds recovered chemical value.
