                                                                                        The synthesis of hybrid cellulose acetate/SiO2/MOF membranes was
                                                                                    successfully carried out with MOF contents of 1, 1.5, 2 and 2.5 %.
                                                                                        The membranes characterization showed that the addition of UiO-66
                                                                                    (Zr) and the increase in its content resulted in higher hydraulic perme­
                                                                                    abilities from 14.38 to 33.82 kg/h/m2/bar and in higher MWCOs
                                                                                    ranging from 3.5 to 14.7 kDa.
                                                                                        In the permeation tests with pCS, it was also possible to observe a
                                                                                    decrease in the pCS rejection coefficient for membranes with a higher
                                                                                    MOF content, concluding that the CA22/SiO2/UiO-66(Zr) 1.5 % mem­
                                                                                    branes, CA22/SiO2/UiO-66(Zr) 2 % and CA22/SiO2/UiO-66(Zr) 2.5 %
                                                                                    will be the best to use for filtering solutions with pCS, as they combine
                                                                                    the lowest toxin rejection values with the highest hydraulic
                                                                                    permeabilities.
