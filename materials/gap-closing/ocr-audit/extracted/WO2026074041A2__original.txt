(57) Abstract: The invention relates to a method for recovering cellulose acetate
from cellulose acetate-containing post-consumer mouthpieces and/or post-consumer
filters, in particular from HT products. In the method, the post-consumer mouth-
pieces and/or post-consumer filters, which are used as starting material, are first me-
chanically pretreated in order to at least partially remove and/or separate foreign sub-
stances and/or foreign bodies from the starting material. Subsequently, the pretreated
starting material is cleaned by means of a treatment using a cleaning agent in such
a way that at least partially remaining impurities are washed off and/or washed out
and/or extracted from the pretreated starting material. Optionally, the cleaning agent
can be recovered, in particular by means of a distillation process.
