## Disclosure versus assumption

| Question | Source-supported disclosure, with location | What remains unresolved for J-H-01 |
|---|---|---|
| Examples or merely an abstract? | A section headed “Description d'exemples de réalisation” describes embodiments and figures, rather than supplying only an abstract (description pp. 5–9) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | Embodiment descriptions are not a measured comparative trial or a validated performance table. |
| Are separated material fractions named? | Grinding/sieving separates filter from tobacco/ash/other organic material; that material is stored for later partial treatment in an unshown water bath, while separated paper is directed toward an unshown recycling stream (description pp. 5–6) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | No analyte-resolved fraction ranking, final acceptance specification or measured reject-treatment outlet is supplied there. |
| Are washing stages distinguished? | Filter/paper separation and subsequent depollution washing have separate functions; repeated washing and multiple depollution vessels are embodiments, and claim 1 distinguishes the functions (description pp. 3–6; claims 1–3) ([description and claims](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | This does not disclose the proposed decision to segregate an analytically ranked initial-wash fraction as a particular final-treatment candidate. |
| Is water the exclusive disclosed medium? | Water is preferred, but the description also names acetone/other solvent variants, and claim 4 addresses solvent use (description pp. 4, 6–7; claim 4) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | The aqueous J-H-01 candidate is a selected hypothesis boundary, not proof the broader patent is water-only or that any variant passes a product gate. |
| Is treatment specified beyond “recycling water”? | Coagulation, flocculation and settling/clariflocculation are explicitly described; treated water is stored and returned to washing, and claims 5–6 address closed-loop recycling and coagulation/flocculation (description pp. 4, 7; claims 5–6) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | It would be incorrect to say the full patent discloses no treatment mechanism; measured inlet/outlet contaminant performance, residual chemical loading and analyte-specific recycle closure are still `parameter-not-located` in this read. |
| Is a measured purge or bleed defined? | Water recycle and its treatment are described; the reviewed text does not identify a quantified, analyte-controlled purge or an acceptance-based purge-sizing rule (description p. 7; claim 5) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | The measured-purge rule, inventory-growth test and routing decision remain new assumptions/evidence requirements of J-H-01, not demonstrated functions. |
| Is sludge/final residual fate closed? | Settling/floc formation is described; Figure 3 includes an unlabeled outlet arrow beneath the recycler, so it must not be erased from interpretation ([drawing and description](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/9f/56/9b/08c269c1f6f82c/FR3066128B1.pdf)). | A drawn or implied exit is not a characterized sludge, mass, destination, permitted recipient or inlet-to-outlet assay; final residual disposal remains unresolved. |
| Are analytical results demonstrated? | The description qualitatively says water analysis shows decreasing harmful substances and calls the recycled water/free fibre depolluted; the read text does not accompany that statement with an analyte table, assay method, reporting limit or full mass balance (description p. 3) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | Preserve it as a patent assertion, not zero contaminants, detoxification, or a measured product/treatment pass. |
| Is product use defined? | Plastic sheets and several article uses are envisaged, with optional thermocompression (description pp. 4, 8–9; claims 7–8) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | No declared-use contaminant/leaching/emissions acceptance result or separately passed product gate is supplied. |
| Is comparable energy/cost established? | The water-recycler paragraph contains energy wording, but its power/energy units are internally unsuitable for direct adoption as a whole-system intensity (description p. 7) ([full text](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). | Do not silently repair the units or convert that component assertion into cost or energy per dry-feed/conforming-output mass; the comparison remains open. |

## Effect on the synthesis

Source-supported update: the full granted text is more informative than the earlier abstract-only F1 reconstruction, particularly about clariflocculation, treated-water storage, separated-organic handling and solvent variants ([FR3066128B1](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR3066128B1/fr)). Inference: J-H-01 should not be framed as introducing staged washing or water treatment into an otherwise treatment-free disclosure.

New-assumption boundary: J-H-01 still requires measured analyte partition ranking, the conditional choice of reject versus liquid, a measured purge/inventory rule where needed, explicit final residual acceptance and demonstrated treatment performance, and an independent product-use gate. Nothing in this retrieval establishes a four-field sink, closes metals fate or displaces F3 minority dissent.

The document-access question is substantially answered for the mirror-hosted granted facsimile; direct office-hosted/register verification remains a separate documentary check. The scientific and regulatory performance gaps remain open, and any later update should be a versioned addendum rather than an overwrite of accepted evidence.
