## Reusable focused review prompts

Use these only with an attached, identified document. Require exact printed page/section/table references and actual URLs, original units/denominators, reading coverage, and `parameter-not-located` for missing fields; separate source statements, measured results, inference and new assumptions.

- **D01 prompt:** Compare FR3066128B1's embodiments/claims/drawings with J-H-01: staged washing, initial-wash segregation, clariflocculation, recycle, purge, solids/reject destinations, treatment outlet and product gate. Do not infer novelty or successful depollution.
- **D02 prompt:** Extract report identifiers, sample population, chemical versus bioassay classification reasoning, analytic coverage and limits, and the date-specific jurisdictional context. Do not convert historical recommendations into current legal advice.
- **D03 prompt:** Map feed/liquor/pulp/precipitate/reuse samples, quantities and dry/wet denominators; nicotine/TSNA/metals methods and limits; measured treatment/reuse outcomes and unmeasured residual destinations. Do not mistake nondetection or COD removal for a closed fate balance.
- **D04 prompt:** Compare granted claims with the description/examples in original Chinese, retaining phase/solid terminology and the precise recovery denominator. Flag translation ambiguity without supplying operating directions.
- **D05 prompt:** Identify distinct thermal stages and capture/residual boundaries in F2, and extraction/collection/recovery embodiments in F4. Separate patent options from actual-operated evidence.
- **D06 prompt:** Extract original-language cleaning/recovery agents, feed class, treatment/residual boundaries and any measured polymer/contaminant evidence. Keep heated-tobacco and smoked-cigarette evidence separate.
- **D07 prompt:** Identify whether the document is original assay, commentary, company summary or full LCA; extract matrix/analytes for assays and functional unit/inventory/comparator/critical review for LCA. Do not inherit marketing conclusions.
- **D08 prompt:** Identify issuer, recipient/material/use, jurisdiction, validity/edition, analytical scope, acceptance basis, outlet and residual route. A logo, declaration or permit is not treatment-performance evidence.

## Tool suggestions

Start with direct public PDFs and existing saved text; no additional scraper installation is needed for the files already acquired. Use mirror HTML to locate and search text, but verify critical passages on the original facsimile; OCR with suitable language data is a follow-up for image-only pages, not a replacement for checking them.

For lawful licensed access, your EKB account may be useful, but resource-specific entitlements must be checked rather than assumed. If access requires your sign-in, sign in yourself and supply the downloaded PDF or use an approved browser handoff; never send passwords or session cookies in chat, and do not assume licensed files may be redistributed in a public GitHub repository.
