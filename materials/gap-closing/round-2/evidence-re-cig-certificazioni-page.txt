 Certifications and Qualifications 
 We aim to offer our partners and customers the highest level of assurance in terms of process transparency, operational safety, and full compliance with applicable environmental regulations. Each certification represents our concrete commitment to excellence and the legal protection of our stakeholders.

 National Qualifications and Authorizations 
 Registration in the National Register of Environmental Managers 
 Details 
 Registration carried out according to the categories set out in Resolution no. 5 of July 2019. 
 Why we have it 
 To ensure maximum compliance with waste transportation regulations, we are authorized to handle CER 20.03.99 (cigarette butts), ensuring that every phase of the logistics process is conducted in full compliance with current regulations.

 Single Environmental Authorization 
 Details 
 Issued by APPA (Autonomous Province of Trento) for waste recovery facilities. 
 Why we have it 
 This is the fundamental certification that allows our facility to process and recycle cigarette butts. It guarantees customers that the transformation process takes place in an officially recognized and legally compliant facility.

 Analysis and Reporting Tools 
 Process LCA (Life Cycle Assessment) 
 Details 
 Monitoring of CO2 emissions along the entire supply chain (from transportation to production). 
 Why we have it 
 We want to certify the actual CO2 savings of our recycling process compared to traditional disposal alternatives. This tool allows our customers to obtain reliable data to include in their NFD (Sustainability Report) , concretely enhancing their ESG commitment. 
 Choosing Re-Cig means relying on a partner whose cornerstones are regulatory compliance and transparency. Each of our certifications guarantees legal and environmental protection for your business, transforming waste into a certified resource. 
 Types of waste managed (disposal) 
 To ensure the effectiveness of the circular economy process and the quality of the recovered plastic material, our plant is technically structured to treat specific waste categories. 
 What can we recover? 
 The Re-Cig process is optimized for the recovery and transformation of: 
 Traditional packaged cigarettes: butts derived from standard industrial products. 
 Heated Tobacco Products (HTP): Butts from new-generation combustion-free devices. 
 Hand-rolled cigarettes: as long as they have a filter. 
 Technical exclusions 
 Electronic cigarettes (liquid e-cigs): These devices cannot be disposed of in our system as they are classified as electronic waste (WEEE) and lack the filter component compatible with our recycling process.
