### 1d. Is there a full LCA/ACV (functional unit · system inventory · comparator · independent critical review)?

**NOT FOUND — explicit statement.** No Keenat public document contains an ISO 14040/14044‑type ACV with a functional unit, a system inventory, a quantified comparator (e.g., vs. incineration or vs. CSS) and an independent critical review. Specifically:

- **The 2026 "Guide Recyclage vs valorisation"** is a **company self‑published livre blanc**. Its public landing page describes "une **analyse comparative** complète sur le cas du mégot… Données industrielles réelles… 6 critères… grille de décision issue de l'Analyse de Cycle de Vie (ACV, normes ISO 14040 et 14044)" — i.e., it *distills* an ACV-style decision framework; it is not an LCA study, is not published in full on the web, and was not downloaded here. (User already possesses it.)
- **INERIS‑DRC‑17‑162405‑01313C (2017)** — the only independent technical study Keenat publishes — contains ecotoxicological testing (D. magna, V. fischeri, P. subcapitata, C. dubia, higher plants, earthworms, A. globiformis), chemical analyses and mass balance on 3 samples (16BB634/635/636), toxicity classification (**HP 14 écotoxique**, **HP 6**; **UN 2811, packing group III** for transport), a hazard‑driven comparison of *stockage / incinération / recycling*, and concludes that "sur la base du peu d'éléments disponibles, notamment concernant la performance des procédés de lavage des filtres, il est aujourd'hui difficile… d'évaluer leur pertinence". It has **no functional unit, no system inventory, no quantified comparator (no CO₂/energy/water balances), no critical review** — it is a **hazard/classification and pathway‑screening study, not an LCA/ACV**. Keyword scan of the extracted text for `unité fonctionnelle|inventaire|ISO 1404x|revue critique|cycle de vie|ACV|comparaison` returned **no LCA hits**.
- **EVAPLUS (2024)** — independent, ADEME‑commissioned, but on **healthcare single‑use plastics**, not mégots. It *recommends* ACV as a tool for REP filières (mentions at several places) and contains **no worked LCA** with functional unit/system/comparator/critical review for mégots or any other waste stream.
- **Keenat's own web content repeatedly cites "Analyses de Cycle de Vie"** (e.g., `/recyclage-valorisation-megots/`: "selon les Analyses de Cycle de Vie"; "l'analyse du cycle de vie complet a démontré que la valorisation énergétique en CSS a un impact environnemental global plus faible") — always without publishing the underlying study. A site search for `life cycle` and a targeted web search (`site:keenat.com filetype:pdf ACV OR "cycle de vie" OR LCA OR ecobilan`) surfaced only the marketing pages above and the EVAPLUS PDF.
- **English pages:** `keenat.com/en/*` does not exist — everything 301‑redirects to French URLs; the phrase "life cycle" on Keenat.com exists only inside French pages ("cycle de vie").

**Bottom line (Target 1):** only marketing guides exist publicly; the one independent study is a 2017 hazard/pathway assessment, not a full LCA. **A full LCA/ACV with functional unit, system inventory, comparator and independent critical review is NOT FOUND for Keenat.** 

*Caveats:* (a) the full keenat.com sitemap crawl was infeasible in bounded time (started; exceeded 15 min with zero bytes logged; killed — recorded here for honesty) and was replaced by targeted site searches + scoped web searches; (b) the two gated landing pages could conceal nothing more than the two guides — they expose no direct file URLs; (c) INERIS report conclusion above is quoted from its `10. CONCLUSION` section (extracted text, p. 46–47/92).
