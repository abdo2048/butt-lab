## 4. Key finding — heavy metals are not tested

The EoW product criteria contain **no heavy-metal parameters** (no Pb, Cd, Hg, As, Ni, Cr or any metal screen). A lot can legally pass the Italian EoW gate with zero metal analysis. Consequences:

- This does **not** contradict the batch-01 metals stop-condition; it demonstrates that the Italian regulatory gate is **narrower than the project's evidence requirement**.
- Any claim that "EoW certification proves contaminant removal" is unsupported. The EoW gate certifies polymer identity and physical properties, not decontamination performance.
- The per-lot DDCs and laboratory analyses remain at the company and are not public — so even the criteria that do exist have no publicly verifiable performance data behind them.

## 5. Discrepancies and cautions

- **CER vs EER code:** the published Albo Nazionale registration (renewal, 09/05/2025, valid to 11/09/2030) lists CER 20.03.99; Re-Cig's own 2026-06 news post claims the registration was updated to EER 20.01.99 with APPA assessing the change as non-substantial (notification 25/05/2026). The published PDF predates the change. Both recorded unchanged.
- **Albo scope wording:** Art. 1 limits activity to green/cemetery waste (Category 1, Class F) while Art. 2 lists four vehicles for CER 20.03.99 smoking waste — the internal wording difference is unresolved in the document itself.
- **Scale:** 100 t/a overall ceiling is small; relevant to the S-gap on scale (a single plant at this ceiling is a pilot-scale operation relative to French collection volumes).
- ISO 14001 (ICIM, IT-150401, exp. 2028-08-26) and ISO 45001 are management-system certificates, not treatment-performance evidence.
- The 2024 Sustainability Report is company self-published.

## 6. Implications for the batch-01 record

- **J-H-01:** unchanged — remains an unvalidated hypothesis.
- **R-gaps:** this AUT is the only publicly verifiable European regulatory acceptance route for cigarette-butt recycling in the corpus (Italian provincial level, waste-code + EoW framework + product spec + per-lot declaration). It is evidence that a legal route **exists**, not evidence that decontamination **works**.
- **Sink framework:** the product-side acceptance basis is now documented; incoming-waste and residue routes are covered by the AUT chain but performance data are not public.
- **Metals stop-condition:** strengthened, not weakened — the strongest available regulatory gate does not test for metals, so the stop-condition must remain in force for any future pilot.
