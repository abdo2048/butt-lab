## 0. Summary card
TerraCycle (Trenton, NJ, founded by Tom Szaky) ran the first worldwide cigarette-butt collection and recycling program from 2012, first sponsored by Santa Fe Natural Tobacco, later rolled out to Australia (2014), Canada and France (Zero Waste Boxes). The disclosed outline: collected butts are shredded; tobacco and paper are composted; the cellulose-acetate filters are dried, powdered/refined into pellets, and compounded by contracted third-party processors into industrial plastics — shipping pallets, plastic lumber, benches, ashtrays. The company explicitly restricted outputs to industrial products, not household plastics, because of nicotine exposure. Priority on the registry because it is the only route documented at city/multi-country logistics scale. But: the actual sanitize/decontamination chemistry is trade-secret and mineable from no patent; every process number (temperatures, tonnages, % recycled content) is `parameter_not_located` in the public record; and the operator's credibility is dented by a settled false-advertising suit (Jan Dell, 2021) and the Bloomberg 2022 investigation into its contracted-processor model — which the team has now READ IN FULL (v0.2): TerraCycle does no recycling itself (all reprocessing contracted out, no independent recycling audit until 2021), revenue $71M (2021, company-stated), contaminated flakes downcycled into playground surfaces/outdoor furniture, and tracker evidence of collected material ending in landfill. ## 4. Safety notes
- **The route's own output is nicotine-bearing**: the operator restricted end uses because of nicotine exposure — treat all pellets/lumber from this route as chemically contaminated, not clean. [S2]
- **Compost stream is hazardous**: tobacco/paper compost carries nicotine, TSNAs, metals — this is not clean garden compost; lawful disposal/treatment boundary required (registry F1 sink rule applies). [S1][S2]
- **Stored used butts** are a pathogen/odor burden; any handling needs hygiene controls; the sanitize step being unknown means no assumed safety. [S1]
- **No DIY replication**: nothing here is a recipe; the only safe team activity is logistics study and product assay of existing outputs.
- **Stop-condition**: metals uncharacterised (row 10).
- Desk research authorizes no physical trial; qualified facility, risk assessment, lawful waste handling, named human actor.
