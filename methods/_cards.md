## C-01 cnr-mask-filters
CNR (Italy) patented crushing butts and recovering cellulosic material for respiratory-mask filters (IT202000003745A1, WO2021170381A1, EP3892393A1). Registry holds only the abstract-level "crush, separate, recover" disclosure — no process numbers extracted. `parameter_not_located`: disinfection chemistry, fiber specs, filtration performance, whether mask-grade material ever passed any standard. Family is pandemic-era (2020 priority). Status: pending_deep_dive. Verify: full EP3892393A1 spec read; any commercialization trace.

## C-02 cerdia-ca-recovery
Cerdia International (DE, filter-maker) filed WO2026074041A2 (2024 priority) for recovering cellulose acetate from post-consumer filters including heat-not-burn mouthpieces. `ai_inference`: industrially the most credible CA-recovery claimant in the registry because the applicant already operates acetate tow plants. `parameter_not_located`: full spec unextracted (newest filing on the shelf). Status: pending_deep_dive. Verify: full WO spec read — the single highest-value unextracted shelf item.

## C-15 kr-microbial-composting
KR10-1782684-1 (granted 2018): microbial composting device with odor removal. `ai_inference`: biodegradation of CA is slow/unproven (CA's acetyl groups resist microbial attack; litter studies show decades-long persistence) — the patent's decomposition claim needs strains, time, temperature data, none extracted. Status: pending_deep_dive.

## C-16 academic-green-ethanol-wash
Peer-reviewed staged water/ethanol CA cleaning: hot water 50 degC 60 min -> 3x cold rinse -> 2x 99% ethanol; output clean CA (PMC7659942). No known patent block. This is the most lab-replicable open cleaning recipe in the registry — candidate to fold into any team washing train (M-01/M-02 adjacency). Status: pending_deep_dive but recipe-level already public.

## C-17 academic-acetic-acid-route

## C-18 academic-ca-membranes

## C-19 cigarette-butt-asphalt
RMIT line: bitumen-encapsulated CBs in stone-mastic asphalt; 10-15 kg CB/m3; encapsulation (105 degC/24 h dry, 150 degC bitumen pot) required because CBs are toxic; strength decreases with CB content. Registry cites ceramics.org + Mohajerani 2017. Shares the M-08 leaching logic (encapsulation = the same bitumen pre-test seen in Kurmus Ch.5). Status: pending_deep_dive; verify: Marshall stability numbers, long-term road performance.

## C-20 concrete-additive
Butts as concrete additive (unach.mx source). Registry-level only; `ai_inference`: organic inclusion in concrete is water-absorption/degradation-risk territory; without a specific study read, treat as weakest construction card. Status: pending_deep_dive.

## C-21 3d-printing-filament
Filaret (EE startup) + academic: recovered CA to 3D-printing filament (3dprinting.com coverage). `parameter_not_located`: filament specs, printing parameters, decontamination train. `ai_inference`: CA needs plasticizer/drying control for FDM — plausible but unproven. Status: pending_deep_dive.

## C-22 cnr-knowledge-share-ca-recovery
CNR + AzzeroCO2 patent OFFERING (knowledge-share.eu): acetone dissolution of CA + nicotine extraction; filtration of paper/ash/tobacco; water anti-solvent precipitation of CA; acetone recovery by boiling; pollutant-water treatment; claimed reuse of the solid for spectacle frames, masks, shields, strings. Status: experimentally-supported-in-source (archive/dossier evidence exists), but offering-stage: TRL unverified. `parameter_not_located`: acetone-to-butt ratio, anti-solvent ratio, yield, purity, economics. Overlaps M-02 (De Fenzo acetone line) and C-01 (CNR mask use) — treat as the CNR ecosystem's technology-transfer face.
