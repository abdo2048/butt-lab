# Egypt annex: unvalidated observations and unvalidated hypotheses

**Status: proposed — desk research only.** This annex is a scoping document, not a feasibility study, and it makes no recommendation about what should be done in Egypt. It was written from the same accepted desk record as the rest of the site, and no measurement, sample or field observation was made for it.

## What this annex is, and what it is not

It is a statement of what is believed to be true in Egypt about cigarette-butt litter, what is merely hypothesised about which recycling routes could apply, and what is not known at all.

It is not a litter census, not a regulatory review, not an engineering study, and not a cost estimate.

## Cigarette-butt litter in Egypt — unvalidated observations

- Cigarette-butt litter is observed in urban areas, near building entrances, and in other public spaces. This observation is not backed by a validated Egypt-specific litter census.
- No validated butt-specific collection or take-back programme was identified.
- Disposal routes are presumed to enter the municipal solid waste stream or to remain in litter streams. No validated data was located for either.

The word presumed is doing real work in that last bullet. Nothing there was measured.

## Regulatory context — unvalidated observations

- No validated end-of-waste criteria and no validated product-quality criteria were identified for this material in this jurisdiction.
- No validated waste classification was identified.
- General waste regulations may apply, but their applicability to this material was not validated.

## The metals stop-condition applies here

The metals stop-condition is in force regardless of geographic context. A stream with uncharacterised heavy-metal content cannot be routed to a product or to unclassified waste, and cadmium, lead, arsenic and mercury are an explicit stop condition rather than something that can be averaged away.

No stream may leave laboratory scale without demonstrated inlet-to-outlet analytical performance.

## The four-field sink applies here too

No method family demonstrates all four required fields anywhere, and therefore none demonstrates them in Egypt.

## What this annex does not claim

- It does not claim that any route is preferable to any other.
- It does not claim that litter is collected, counted, or disposed of in any particular way.
- It does not claim that any product made from this material would meet any standard.
- It does not claim that the regulatory picture described here is complete.
