# Start here: what cigarette-butt recycling is, and how to read the rest of this site

This page is a map of the site. Everything here was assembled by desk research: the material was read from published sources, patents, company material and the project's own accepted records, and no measurement was made for this project at any point.

## What a cigarette butt actually is

A spent cigarette butt is a layered composite, not a single material. It reaches us from ash trays, bins and street litter in four recognisable parts, and each part carries a different contamination load.

- **Cellulose acetate filter tow.** The main structural body of the filter, and the part most recycling routes want back.
- **Paper wrapper.** Carries combustion residue from the burning process.
- **Tobacco rod.** The remaining tobacco, which retains tobacco-specific compounds.
- **Fine ash.** The smallest fraction, and the one most easily lost during handling.

The consequence that runs through every method on this site: because the layers carry different contaminants, a process that treats the butt as one material will either leave some contaminants untouched, or redistribute them without removing them.

The contaminant set the records track is nicotine, tobacco-specific nitrosamines, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons, tar and combustion residues, cadmium, lead, arsenic, mercury, and combustion salts.

## The six families published here

| Family | What it does | Where the material goes |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical separation with aqueous washing | Separates and washes the filter from the rest of the butt | Recovered filter tow, plus wet solid and liquid residuals |
| Dry treatment and composite extrusion | Dries and thermally treats the butt, then compounds it | Extruded composite profile |
| Solvent dissolution and polymer recovery | Dissolves the acetate and recovers it from solution | Recovered polymer, plus solvent and liquor residuals |
| Biological treatment | Uses microbial or enzymatic action to break the material down | Compost-like or fractionated outputs |
| Material incorporation into other products | Puts the recovered material into another product's matrix | Filler or fibre in a host material |
| Product routes | Surveys where recovered material could go at all | A map of possible destinations rather than a process |

Two of these six are best read as surveys rather than treatments: material incorporation and product routes describe where material could go, while the other four describe ways material is treated.

## How the evidence is labelled

Records that make an evidence claim carry two labels, and the site never hides either one. Sources, terms, method families and the material record carry the role they play instead of an evidence state.

**How well the evidence is supported.** A claim, assessment, gap or hypothesis carries an evidence state: supported by a located source, sources disagree, no located source, reasoned from located sources, an AI proposal, an AI gap-closing note, or unverified. Every assessment also carries its basis: sourced, inferred, or AI-reviewed.

**How far the claim has been validated.** An assessment carries a result: scored, unknown, or not applicable. A gap needs human validation. A hypothesis is new and unvalidated.

The second label is the one to read first: every hypothesis on this site is a new and unvalidated hypothesis, and every gap still needs human validation.

Where the research assistant has added reasoning that goes beyond what a source states, it is marked inline as an AI inference or an AI proposal. Where a number could not be located, it is left as parameter-not-located rather than filled with a plausible figure. A missing number is not a zero, and it is not a licence to invent one.

## Why material fate and product quality are two different questions

This is the most common confusion in this field, and the records keep the two apart deliberately.

**Material fate** asks where the contaminants went. For each contaminant and each stream: the product, every liquid and solid residual, captured emissions and condensate, purge, treatment inlet and final outlet, plus the inventory at the beginning and end of a recycle cycle.

**Product quality** asks whether the recovered material is fit for a declared use in a declared jurisdiction. That means leaching and migration, emissions, and material performance, judged against a stated acceptance criterion.

A closed mass balance can describe complete retention of a contaminant inside a product. That is a mass-balance result, and it is not a product safety result. Likewise, a treatment permit is not proof of treatment performance, and a management-system certificate is not proof of product performance.

A recovered material is only a product when four things are located: a dry-mass balance, an analyte-resolved fate for the contaminants, a named final route with an acceptance basis, and a product gate that does not depend on the balance closing.

## The four fields, and the metals stop-condition

Every method on this site is judged against the same four fields, and every one of them currently fails at least one.

1. **Dry-mass balance.** One common dry-feed-basis balance across every stream, with unexplained loss kept explicit.
2. **Analyte-resolved fate.** Per-analyte mass in the product, in every residual, in captured emissions, in purge, and in treatment inlet and final outlet.
3. **Treatment route and acceptance.** A named actual final route, a documented acceptance of the characterised stream, and measured inlet-to-outlet analytical performance including generated solids and air emissions.
4. **Use-specific product gate.** A declared intended use and jurisdiction, judged before contaminant content, leaching, emissions and material performance are considered, with a gate that does not depend on the mass balance closing.

**The metals stop-condition is in force across all of it.** A stream with uncharacterised heavy-metal content cannot be routed to a product or to unclassified waste. Cadmium, lead, arsenic and mercury are an explicit stop condition rather than a parameter to be averaged away, so a favourable result in one field cannot offset uncharacterised metals. The trigger is any detectable metal above background in the feedstock, the wash water, the air emission, a solid residue or the final product; the required action is to halt the stream, identify the source, and demonstrate inlet-to-outlet analytical performance before resuming.

## How to compare the families

Each family page carries the same eight criteria: evidence strength, process ease, resource burden, contaminant-fate coverage, sink completeness, product readiness, regulatory clarity, and validation maturity.

Three rules apply to that comparison.

- **Unknown is a result.** A criterion is marked unknown when the evidence needed was not found. It is not a low score and it is not a zero.
- **The criteria are not averaged.** There is no overall score and no default ranking, because averaging a missing value into a present one would invent a conclusion the evidence does not support.
- **A favourable field never substitutes for another.** Each field stands on its own.

If you would rather see the gaps first than the strengths, the site's compare view can order the families by how much is still unknown rather than by how much is established.

## Where the invention lives

The new method proposals are published as hypothesis records, not as process descriptions. There are three of them.

- **The invention sketch.** The batch-02 synthesis that set out the four-field sink failure map and the metals stop-condition.
- **The scientist-inventor proposals.** Five falsifiable engineering variants, each pairing an upstream pre-fractionation step with a downstream polishing step, and each carrying executable simulations.
- **The evidence-gap synthesis.** A cross-family system inventory and life-cycle-assessment gate covering the remaining measurement, regulatory and scale gaps.

All three are marked as new and unvalidated hypotheses. None of them has been tested, and the site presents them as candidates for a trained reader to judge, reject or rewrite.

## What this site does not contain

- No measurement, pilot or field trial was carried out, and none is authorised by this site.
- No independent, public, full life-cycle assessment of a commercial butt-recycling process was located.
- No patentability, freedom-to-operate, regulatory or commercial determination is made anywhere.
- No claim of cleanliness, detoxification, safety, commercial validation, environmental superiority, novelty or readiness to deploy is made for any family or proposal.
