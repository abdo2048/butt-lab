# Egypt Annex — Cigarette Butt Recycling Context

**Status: proposed — desk research only**

This annex records contextual information about cigarette-butt recycling in
Egypt. It is part of Batch-02 Phase 1, which is desk research only. No
experiment has been run or authorized. No claim of cleanliness, safety,
deployability, cost-effectiveness, or environmental benefit is made or
supported by this document.

## Overview

This annex documents what is known about cigarette-butt litter, collection,
and potential recycling pathways in Egypt. It is a scoping document, not a
feasibility study. The information below is unvalidated and should not be used
as a basis for any pilot or deployment decision without separate operator
authorization and explicit experiment approval.

## Cigarette-butt litter in Egypt — unvalidated observations

This section records unvalidated observations about cigarette-butt litter in
Egypt. Specific figures (litter volumes, collection rates, disposal practices)
are not independently confirmed and are marked as such throughout.

- **Litter presence:** Cigarette butts are observed as litter in urban areas,
  near building entrances, and in public spaces, consistent with global
  patterns. No validated Egyptian-specific litter census was found.
- **Collection infrastructure:** No validated cigarette-butt-specific
  collection or take-back program was identified in publicly available
  sources. General waste collection exists but butt-specific streams are not
  documented.
- **Disposal routes:** Cigarette butts are presumed to enter municipal solid
  waste or litter streams, but no validated data on Egyptian disposal routes
  was found.

## Regulatory context — unvalidated observations

No validated analysis of Egyptian waste-regulation, product-approval, or
environmental-permitting requirements for cigarette-butt recycling was found.
The following are unvalidated observations:

- No validated Egyptian-specific end-of-waste or product-quality criteria for
  recycled cigarette-butt material was identified.
- No validated waste-classification for cigarette-butt feedstock in Egypt was
  found.
- General waste-management and environmental regulations may apply, but their
  applicability to butt-recycling is unvalidated.

## Metals stop-condition applies

The metals stop-condition established in Batch-01 and restated in the
Batch-02 invention sketch (`reports/batch-02/invention.md`) applies to any
consideration of cigarette-butt recycling in Egypt:

- Any detectable metal above background in feedstock, wash water, air
  emission, solid residue, or final product triggers a stop.
- No stream may leave laboratory scale without demonstrated analytical
  performance (inlet-to-outlet).

This condition is in force regardless of geographic context.

## Four-field sink applies to Egypt

The four-field sink (dry-mass closure, analyte-resolved contaminant fate,
use-specific product gate, metals stop-condition) established in Batch-01
applies to any geographic context, including Egypt. No method family reviewed
in Batch-02 demonstrates all four fields anywhere, let alone in Egypt.
