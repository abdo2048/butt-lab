## Proposed four-field sink accounting

Every method family reviewed in Batch-02 fails at least one field of the
four-field sink:

1. **Dry-mass closure** — none of the six families demonstrates a complete
   dry-mass balance with analyte-resolved accounting.
2. **Analyte-resolved contaminant fate** — no family shows where each measured
   analyte goes through every process step (water, air, solid residue, product).
3. **Use-specific product gate** — no family sets an independent product-quality
   gate separate from mass-balance closure.
4. **Metals stop condition** — no family treats metals as an explicit stop
   condition with a demonstrated analytical test.

This invention sketch does not claim to solve any of the four fields. It
proposes treating the four-field sink as a mandatory prerequisite for any later
authorized pilot, not as a solved problem.

## Metals stop-condition

A metals stop-condition must be in force before any process stream leaves the
laboratory bench:

- **Trigger:** any detectable metal above background in feedstock, wash water,
  air emission, solid residue, or final product.
- **Required action:** halt the stream; identify source; demonstrate analytical
  performance (inlet-to-outlet) before resuming.
- **Justification:** batch-01 identified metals as an explicit stop condition
  that remains in force.

This sketch does not propose how to remove metals. It proposes that metals are
a stop condition, not a solvable parameter within this sketch.
