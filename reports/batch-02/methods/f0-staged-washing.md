# Method Family F0 — Staged Water/Ethanol Washing

## Overview
F0 is a staged liquid-washing process that removes contaminants from cigarette butts using sequential washes in water and/or ethanol. Butts are collected, dried if wet, then passed through one or more wash stages—typically water first to remove water-soluble residues (nicotine, tar components, salts), followed optionally by ethanol to extract more lipophilic residues from the cellulose acetate filter material. The washed filter material is then dried and may be milled into a fibrous form for downstream use. F0 is positioned at the simpler end of mechanical/physical processing: no solvents beyond water and ethanol, no dissolution step, no chemical reaction intended.

## Mechanism
1. **Collection and drying**: Spent cigarette butts are collected and dried to remove ambient moisture.
2. **Water wash stage**: Butts are agitated in water (often heated, sometimes with agitation) to leach out water-soluble components—nicotine, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbon residues carried by tar, some combustion salts, and paper fibers.
3. **Optional ethanol wash stage**: A second wash in ethanol extracts ethanol-soluble residues (oils, waxes, some tar components) that water cannot remove.
4. **Rinse**: A final rinse removes residual wash liquor.
5. **Drying**: The washed filter material is dried, typically at moderate temperature.
6. **Size reduction**: The dried material may be milled to a fibrous or particulate form.

The goal is to produce a cellulose-acetate-rich fibrous material with reduced contaminant load, suitable for incorporation into composite products or further processing.

## Feedstock Requirements
- Spent cigarette butts (filter + paper + residual tobacco, tobacco removed or retained depending on target product)
- Drying capacity (oven or air-drying area)
- Wash vessels with agitation
- Water supply; optionally ethanol (food-grade or denatured, recovered where possible)
- Filtration for spent wash liquor
- Drying oven (moderate temperature, typically 60–100 °C)
- Milling equipment (if fibrous product is desired)

## Contaminant Fate
- **Nicotine**: Partially removed in the water wash; residual nicotine may remain bound to filter material or paper.
- **Tar and combustion residues**: Partially extracted by water and ethanol; not all components are removed.
- **Heavy metals (cadmium, lead, arsenic)**: Partially leached into wash liquor; not proven removed to safe levels for product contact.
- **Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs)**: Partially extracted; ethanol more effective than water for lipophilic PAHs.
- **Microplastics**: The cellulose acetate filter itself is a microplastic precursor; washing does not eliminate this.
- **Wash liquor**: Contaminated with extracted toxins; requires wastewater treatment, not disposal to drain.
