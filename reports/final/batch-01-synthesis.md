# Batch-01 cigarette-butt recycling: final desk synthesis

Date: 2026-11-07. Scope: batch-01 only, process families F0–F5, accepted Stage-3 evidence review and accepted Stage-4 hypothesis generation. **Desk synthesis only: no experimental validation, no safety claim, no novelty claim, and no experiment authorized.** This report makes no patentability, freedom-to-operate, legal, deployment, or environmental-benefit determination.

## Synthesis position and decision boundary

The best-supported conclusion is an evidence boundary, not a successful recycling process: no family is proven clean, safe, deployable, or environmentally beneficial, and none jointly establishes dry-mass closure, analyte-resolved fate, final treatment performance, and an independently passed use-specific product gate ([accepted Stage-3 synthesis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-3/round-01/judge.md)). The strongest individual Stage-4 proposal is B-P-01; the primary revision target is **J-H-01, a new unvalidated judge hypothesis**, combining its F1-based partition-ranking architecture with measured purge, separate reject/sludge boundaries, treatment acceptance and outlet evidence, and an independent product gate ([Stage-4 judgment](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).

F3 remains the strongest minority alternative when recoverable cellulose-acetate quality is the controlling objective; this is dissent, not a settled winner or a validated comparator ([Stage-4 dissent](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)). Metals remain the hardest analyte class across the proposals because none demonstrates a metals-removal outcome, a use-compliant product, or a complete lawful metals sink ([Stage-4 metals assessment](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).

## Research question, scope, and evidence semantics

The investigation asks which cigarette-butt recycling claims survive the available public evidence and, from that evidence, which modified route could plausibly produce a usable output together with a characterized, lawful toxic-fraction sink, and what would falsify it ([Stage-3 question as resolved](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-3/round-01/judge.md); [Stage-4 question as resolved](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)). This is a purpose-bounded synthesis of the selected batch, not a worldwide inventory, exhaustive prior-art search, or ranking of all recycling technologies.

The evidentiary baseline is Stage-3 packet v1.1 at payload commit `5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774` and Stage-4 packet v1.0 at payload commit `671de7c045385dade4b8f26c7b1680a2cc3a809c`; release metadata and later panel outputs are distinct from those frozen payloads ([accepted workflow record](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/state/workflow.yaml); [Stage-4 manifest](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/packets/batch-01/stage-4/manifest.yaml)). Both operator acceptance statements were read verbatim: acceptance closes the evidence-review and hypothesis-generation handoffs with REVISE carried forward, not the scientific validation of any proposal ([operator acceptances](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/state/workflow.yaml)).

Claims below use the following distinctions. Proposed measurements are evidence requirements, not test results or authorization.

- **Source-supported:** A read reconstruction, panel, or source states the claim; this does not establish reproduction or independent verification.
- **Inference:** A mechanistic or integration interpretation drawn from the record, not a measured outcome.
- **New-assumption:** A performance, partition, routing, or product-use proposition introduced by a worker or judge; it remains falsifiable and unvalidated.
- **Unresolved:** Evidence is missing, access is partial, denominators conflict, or a source has not been read; missing values are `parameter-not-located`, not zero.

Primary texts not obtained in the earlier stages were not newly retrieved for this synthesis. Citations identify the actual read project evidence and its reading limits, not a claim that this session independently read the underlying patents, regulatory texts, or laboratory reports. The judge's working files were read for provenance only, not as additional scientific evidence.

## Dissent and unresolved provenance

- **F3 minority:** The primary F1 choice optimizes testability of sink architecture, not polymer quality; F3 may be preferable under the latter objective, subject to co-precipitation, metals, solvent closure and independent product evidence ([preserved minority position](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Wrong-stream and dilute-effluent objection:** An initial-wash purge may miss the controlling tobacco/ash or product burden and create a larger unroutable liquid; only analyte-specific mass ranking, not concentration alone, discriminates those versions ([B-P-01 adversarial screen](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/worker-b.md)).
- **Metals objection:** No washing, phase-transfer or conversion proposal demonstrates metals compliance; thermal treatment is not elemental destruction, and species-specific fate cannot be replaced with a blanket volatility claim ([judge's metals stop condition](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md); [frozen F2 reasoning limits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774/specs/keenat-fr3110470a1-source-reconstruction.md)).
- **F2 dilution/off-gas warning:** A lower product concentration can arise from matrix dilution while less-volatile contaminants remain and volatile material exits in unmeasured air; this warning is not removed by selecting J-H-01 ([accepted Stage-3 warning](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-3/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Scale objection:** F1 cannot be called cheapest, F5 most expensive or any family deployment-ready from this record; incompatible inventories, functions and throughput bases block those rankings ([Stage-4 scale restraint](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Classification versus product evidence:** Feed hazard characterization is not an automatic legal classification of every output, and a chemical panel does not necessarily resolve all ecotoxicity or exposure endpoints; actual jurisdiction and intended use remain open ([Stage-4 classification distinction](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
