# Batch-01 validation requirements and open questions

Date: 2026-11-07. This is a desk-only evidence agenda accompanying the final batch-01 synthesis, not an experimental protocol or authorization. **No experimental validation, no safety claim, no novelty claim, and no experiment authorized.** No legal, patentability, freedom-to-operate, deployment or environmental-benefit determination is made.

## Priority, evidence status, and independent gates

The primary revision target is **J-H-01, a new unvalidated judge hypothesis**; A-P-01/F3 is the strongest minority comparator when polymer quality controls the objective, while B-P-02 and B-P-03 remain secondary hypotheses rather than validated fallback processes ([accepted Stage-4 judgment](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)). B-P-01 and C-P-01/F1P retain their individual provenance but are represented in the primary agenda through J-H-01's explicitly new combination ([proposal mapping and synthesis](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).

Source-supported means the read record states a mechanism or claim; inference means interpretation without demonstrated performance; new-assumption means an unmeasured performance, routing or usability proposition. Any unspecified numerical criterion, sample count, method performance, price, operating condition or partition value is `parameter-not-located`; no universal threshold is created.

The accepted evidence requirements separate overall dry-mass accounting, analyte fate, final treatment acceptance/performance and product-use performance rather than letting one favorable result substitute for the others ([Stage-3 accepted distinctions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-3/round-01/judge.md)). In particular, a closed balance can describe complete retention in product, and a permit or partner name cannot demonstrate treatment performance ([Stage-4 gate requirements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).

## Shared minimum evidence envelope for any later authorized pilot

The following describes what evidence would be needed, not how to operate equipment. No pressure-vessel, solvent-recovery/distillation, caustic-pulping or hazardous-waste processing instructions are given.

- **Feed and basis:** Define a bounded, traceable feed lot; report received wet mass, moisture/dry-matter basis, and whole-butt versus filter-only basis without silently switching denominators; map representative aliquots to total parent-stream mass or volume ([judge's common feed requirement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Analyte panel:** Report nicotine, identified TSNAs including NNN/NNK where appropriate, representative PAHs, and each of Cd/Pb/As/Hg separately; route-relevant solvents and material-quality evidence are additional requirements, not substitutes ([accepted common panel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-3/round-01/judge.md); [Stage-4 route-specific requirements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Stream totals and inventory:** Measure analyte mass in product, every liquid and solid residual, captured emission/condensate, purge, treatment inlet and final outlet, as well as beginning/end recycle inventory; unexplained loss stays explicit ([judge's all-stream requirement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Analytical interpretability:** Use matrix-appropriate methods with justified sampling, blanks, recovery, uncertainty and detection/quantitation reporting; do not treat below-quantitation results as zero or carry over worker sampling counts and illustrative method-performance values ([judge's measurement corrections](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Product gate:** Declare intended use and jurisdiction before judging contaminant content, leaching/migration, relevant emissions and material performance; any additional ecotoxicity requirement must be addressed separately, and no gate has yet been passed ([independent product gate](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Treatment gate:** Name the actual final route, document acceptance of the characterized stream and measure inlet-to-outlet analytical performance, including generated solids and air emissions; transfer to a treatment boundary alone is insufficient ([four-field sink requirement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).

Synthesis accounting convention, not an executed calculation: for analyte \(i\) in stream \(s\), let \(m_{i,s}\) be total analyte mass, derived from measured concentration and the complete stream mass or volume on the matching basis. The partition fraction is

\[
f_{i,s}=\frac{m_{i,s}}{m_{i,\mathrm{feed}}}.
\]

For a process boundary with opening and closing inventories \(I_{i,0}\) and \(I_{i,1}\), report the unresolved residual as

\[
u_i=m_{i,\mathrm{external\ inputs}}+I_{i,0}
 -m_{i,\mathrm{external\ outputs}}-I_{i,1}.
\]

These are inference-level bookkeeping definitions, not claimed results or acceptance thresholds. Internal recycle, treatment inlet and treatment outlet must not all be summed as independent final outputs; reconcile each sub-boundary separately, then reconcile the whole system. If parent analytes disappear, unexplained disappearance is not automatically detoxification or destruction; transformation evidence and relevant additional endpoints would be needed before making that inference.

## Surviving hypotheses and decisive tests

### J-H-01: primary, new unvalidated F1-based architecture

**Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption:** On a defined feed, analyte partition ranking can identify a controllable reject/purge boundary, with no indefinite recycle accumulation, accepted and analytically demonstrated final treatment, and a CA-rich output that independently meets a declared use gate ([J-H-01 definition](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)). Source-supported F1 separation/washing and an external-partner statement support candidate boundaries only; neither establishes this claim ([frozen F1 reconstruction](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774/specs/mego-fr3066128a1-source-reconstruction.md); [Re-Cig company dossier](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774/specs/company-workflow-dossiers-batch01.md)).

- **Single most decisive measurement:** Same-feed analyte-mass partition ranking across tobacco/ash/reject, candidate initial wash, later wash/purge and CA-rich product; rank total analyte mass per feed-analyte mass, not concentration alone ([B-P-01 discriminator adopted by judge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Minimal later pilot evidence:** Apply the shared panel and dry-feed basis to feed, separated rejects, initial and later wash fractions, opening/closing recycle inventory, measured purge, product, fines, conditional treatment sludge, treatment inlet/outlet and captured drying emissions; independently assess the declared CA-product use and final residual route ([J-H-01 measurement envelope](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Explicit falsifier:** The selected wash-and-purge version is killed if it selects the wrong burden-bearing stream, produces liquid with no acceptable final route, fails demonstrated outlet requirements, or leaves product above its declared use criterion without a measured alternative separation; uncontrolled inventory growth or uncharacterized emissions also prevent support ([judge's failure version](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Decision consequence:** Reject-dominant partition can simplify or redirect J-H-01 rather than prove the whole conditional architecture impossible; retained metals failing the product gate require rejection of that product disposition, not an automatic lower-exposure sale or a claim that a use restriction solves contamination ([judge's redirect and metals conditions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).

### A-P-01 / F3: preserved minority polymer-quality comparator

**Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption:** An additional separation/wash boundary after F3 polymer recovery can reduce retained contaminants while preserving use-relevant CA quality and keeping solvent, liquid, residue and emission streams accountable ([judge's revised A-P-01](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)). F3's family-level recovery logic is source-supported, but the proposed additional-stage selectivity and final treatment outcomes are not ([frozen CN limits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774/specs/cn106928486-source-reconstruction.md)).

- **Single most decisive measurement:** A linked before/after additional-boundary partition-and-polymer-quality balance: quantify analytes and recovered polymer across precipitate, wash/mother liquor and residue, and compare molecular-weight/quality evidence to the defined input and intended product requirement ([A-P-01 falsifiers](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Minimal later pilot evidence:** Apply the shared analyte panel and feed basis to pretreatment liquids, insolubles, precipitated CA, mother liquor, post-recovery wash, recovered solvent, purge, capture streams and final treatment residuals/outlets; separately report residual process solvents/acid, polymer molecular-weight distribution, identity/degree of substitution where relevant, leaching and intended-use material performance ([F3 source gaps](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774/specs/cn106928486-source-reconstruction.md); [judge's A-P-01 requirements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Explicit falsifier:** The added-boundary version fails if co-precipitation/metal retention or residual solvent causes a failed product-use gate, polymer quality cannot meet the declared function, or the recovery/residual system cannot be closed and routed; conductivity or appearance cannot reverse that failure ([judge's A-P-01 disposition](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Dissent boundary:** F3 is not settled by the F1 preference: it may be the stronger starting point under a polymer-quality objective, but Worker A's numerical operating assumptions, arbitrary partition thresholds and apparent measured sink entries are not adopted ([minority preservation and corrections](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).

### B-P-02: secondary metal-first F3 hypothesis

**Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption:** A distinct pre-product liquid phase can remove metals from the CA recovery path with useful selectivity, without unacceptable organic carryover, polymer-quality loss or an unmanageable residual system ([B-P-02 as bounded by judge](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)). Acid-phase metal transfer is an unmeasured premise, not a source-supported outcome of the CN recovery claim ([Worker B premise](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/worker-b.md)).

- **Single most decisive measurement:** A same-feed, phase-resolved balance for each of Cd/Pb/As/Hg across the candidate liquid, recovered CA and filtered insoluble residue; this directly tests the claimed metal destination ([B-P-02 falsifier](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Minimal later pilot evidence:** Use the shared panel and dry-feed basis across every pretreatment, liquid, residue, polymer, wash, recovered-solvent, purge, capture and treatment-outlet stream; add route-relevant speciation evidence, organic co-partition, reagent recovery, residual solvent/acid and the independent CA-use gate ([judge's B-P-02 evidence needs](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Explicit falsifier:** If metals do not leave the polymer path by the hypothesized liquid phase, the metal-first premise fails; a product failing its declared metal criterion or an unroutable metal-bearing residual also kills the proposed disposition ([B-P-02 failure modes](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Inference limit:** Failure of this one liquid-phase premise does not prove every possible CA-recovery process impossible; for example, metals transferred to a separately routable solid would require different accounting and a different hypothesis, not automatic rejection of all polymer recovery.

### B-P-03: secondary F5 liquor-purge and precipitate hypothesis

**Central falsifiable claim, new-assumption:** F5 conversion can be paired with a measured liquor purge and independently characterized precipitate so that contaminants do not accumulate, final liquid/solid routes perform as required, and cellulose product independently meets its intended-use gate ([judge's B-P-03 definition](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)). The source-supported family description does not establish pulp purity, liquor treatability or precipitate disposal ([frozen F5 reading limits](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774/specs/unb-pi0305004-source-reconstruction.md)).

- **Single most decisive measurement:** A cycle-resolved analyte inventory balance across pulp, liquor, purge and precipitate, including treatment outputs; this tests whether the proposed contaminant exit actually prevents accumulation ([B-P-03 loop falsifier](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/worker-b.md); [judge's bounded requirement](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Minimal later pilot evidence:** Use the shared panel and dry whole-butt/filter basis for feed, converted dry pulp, dark liquor, opening/closing loop inventory, wash water, purge, precipitate, captured emissions and final treatment inlet/outlet/residuals; add COD/TOC and relevant salt/phenolic loading without substituting those bulk measures for individual toxicants, and separately assess cellulose identity, function and use-specific leaching/contact evidence ([F5 unresolved streams](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/5f484b585ca16dbd2ce93b56c61c962f5f14c774/specs/unb-pi0305004-source-reconstruction.md); [B-P-03 evidence requirements](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Explicit falsifier:** Continued target-analyte accumulation despite the specified purge/treatment boundary, failed pulp-use criteria or no acceptable final liquor/precipitate route kills the stated version; a large necessary purge would refute an efficient-recycle assumption without alone disproving deacetylation chemistry ([B-P-03 failure distinctions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/worker-b.md); [judge's disposition](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-4/round-01/judge.md)).
- **Document prerequisite:** The Teixeira paper/thesis and primary patent remain necessary to distinguish measured conditions and treatment outcomes from abstract-level retrieval leads; no literature removal number is imported as a design constant ([accepted retrieval-lead limitation](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/abdo2048/butt-lab/4bf6bd8/panels/batch-01/stage-3/round-01/judge.md)).
