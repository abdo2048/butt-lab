**Short Supporting Quotation:**
> "This invention is about a method of recycling tar in cigarette butts containing thousands of different toxic substances harmful to living creatures and nature, by mixing with ethanol, which is used as fuel."

**Disclosed Process Steps (as described in the patent):**
1. **Collecting the cigarette butts** (step 100/200).
2. **Putting cigarette butts into water** (step 110) – 5‑320 g of butts in 1 L water at 0‑60 °C, or **into ethanol** (step 210) – same temperature range, producing a butt‑ethanol mixture of 5‑320 g L⁻¹.
3. **Mixing** (step 120/220) – mechanical or magnetic stirrer, 5‑60 min.
4. **Filtering the mixture** (step 130/240) – using a strainer or micro‑filters, optionally under pressure.
5. **Boiling the mixture** (step 140) – heating tar‑water mixture at 50‑110 °C for 1‑24 h to evaporate water.
6. **Adding mixture to ethanol** (step 150) – combine the tar‑water concentrate with ethanol (1 L ethanol per 5‑320 g tar‑water).
7. **(Optional) Waiting** (step 230) – let the ethanol‑butt mixture stand for ≥15 min to settle precipitates before filtration.

These steps are summarized in Claims 1‑10 of the patent.
