Do not compare 92% patent recovery with 99.7% company fiber/concentrate figures as the same metric. No justified per-tonne margin, universal removal, or environmental ranking follows.

Research priorities: W is the clearest experimental material baseline read, not a certified clean-product process. D tests a reconstitution pathway needing mass/partitioning clarification. P tests recyclable-fluid extraction with incomplete process/performance disclosure. There is no proven winner.

Define product/exposure before pass thresholds. Separate polymer quality/yield from organics, metals, residual fluids, leachate and emissions. Appropriate qualified methods, sample basis, controls and detection limits are needed; odor/transparency/FTIR identity/model agreement do not certify safety.

Proceed to symbolic variables and explicitly hypothetical sensitivity cases, not fabricated measurements. Eliminate candidates whose apparent benefits vanish once residue handling, recycle accumulation, polymer loss and energy are included. Candidate improvements are in inventions/candidate-hypotheses-01.md. No physical trial is authorized.
