# Skill: Claim Labeling

Every quantitative or factual claim in any file MUST carry one label:

[VERIFIED-SOURCE] — a cited primary source directly states it (patent text,
peer-reviewed paper, official company page). Include URL.
[PLAUSIBLE-UNTESTED] — consistent with established science; no direct source.
State the underlying principle.
[MY-ESTIMATE] — bounded guess. State: reasoning, lower bound, upper bound,
what would tighten the range.
[UNSAFE] — danger to humans/environment; requires mitigation or prohibition.

Special markers:
DISPUTED — two agents hold conflicting positions; both positions recorded.
UNRESOLVED — sources conflict (e.g., TchaoMegot press quote 50-60 bar vs
patent 280-320 bar). Flag for resolution, do not silently pick one.

Corollary: fake precision is a violation. "99.4% removal" without a source
is Fabricated until proven otherwise. Ranges beat point estimates.
