## Protocol numbers captured 2026-11-24 (from PMC full text)
- Collection: used filters from ashtrays of a bar/restaurant area in Avellino, provided by Essequadro S.r.l., sent to CNR Portici
- Butts "sterilized and treated" — no parameters disclosed
- External paper removal before extraction — method not detailed
- Hot water wash: 50 degC, 60 min
- Cold water washes: three times
- Ethanol washes: ethanol 99% w/w, twice
- Drying: oven 60 degC, 60 min
- Film: recovered CA dissolved in acetone 5% w/w, cast in petri dish, 2 mm thick, transparent but brittle; plasticizer recommended (identity/dose not given)
- FTIR (recovered CA): 3400, 2920, 2850, 1432, 1739, 1371, 1214, 1100, 1030 cm-1 bands
- TGA/DSC: Tm ~225 degC; Tg 193 degC unused vs 192 degC recovered; max degradation 367 degC; ~85% mass loss (degradative step); ~10% residue; burn time 1.83 min (unused CA); Ea deacetylation 67 kJ/mol (unused) / 61 kJ/mol (recovered) inert, 40 kJ/mol (air), charring 75 kJ/mol; 200 kJ/mol inert 20-80% conversion; 150 kJ/mol air 15-65% conversion
- Not disclosed anywhere in the paper: solvent-to-solid ratios, agitation, stage durations beyond the above, CA yield, contaminant-removal efficiencies, plasticizer details, solvent recovery, costs
