## Relevance note
The most detailed early process account, from named TerraCycle R&D VP Ernie Simpson: filters are melted and blended with other recyclables into plastic pellets for plastic lumber, pallets, bins and ashtrays; ~97% of the filter is cellulose acetate; because the material was exposed to nicotine the pellets are NOT marketed for household products (industrial use only); process development took about six months (vs Plastics News' "nearly two years" — discrepancy noted in M-06); the process began by deconstructing cigarettes and composting the tobacco and paper; testing used butts from TerraCycle's own offices (Albe Zakes).
