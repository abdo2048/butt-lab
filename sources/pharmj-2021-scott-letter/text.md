TerraCycle should be supported and its recycling
scheme reinstated
04 August 2021.




      The views expressed in this letter are those of the author’s. If you would like to

      submit a letter to The Pharmaceutical Journal, please click here

      (mailto:correspondence@pharmaceutical-journal.com) .



Some composite items may be di�cult to recycle, and that includes medication blister

packs.


TerraCycle is a recycling company that has developed a scheme to recycle blister packs

(https://www.terracycle.com/en-GB/brigades/medicine-packet-uk#resources) in the UK.

It was sponsored by Sanoﬁ to handle patient returns and donate some proceeds to charities.

Many pharmacies joined in and the scheme became overloaded, leading to its current

suspension (see The Pharmaceutical Journal’s news story, ‘Medicines packet recycling

scheme scaled back following ‘high uptake” (/article/news/terracycle-scales-back-

medicines-packet-recycling-scheme-after-overwhelming-uptake) , published on 8 July

2021).


It seems to me that TerraCycle should be encouraged to renew the scheme. Pharmacies

should be actively contributing and manufacturers should sponsor the scheme to reduce

waste and support our environment.
I encourage colleagues to use any inﬂuence they have to promote recycling and to sign this

petition (http://chng.it/JYMNgCtn42) , which asks companies to get involved in the funding

of medicine packet recycling schemes.


David Scott
