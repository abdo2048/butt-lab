## Relevance note
CLOSES M-06's sanitize-step row — the only explicit public description of the sterilization step, disclosed by a TerraCycle collection partner: "The collected cigarette waste is first sterilized using Gamma radiation, which removes toxins and bio-contaminants." Then: material shredded, remaining tobacco and paper composted "with strict adherence to tobacco composting laws", shredded filters blended with other recycled materials and pelletized; roughly 150,000 cigarette butts go into one industrial plastic shipping pallet. LCAs conducted by PE International and Five Winds Strategic Consulting showing 20-80% carbon savings. Also litter-scale stats (4.5 trillion butts littered worldwide per year; 65% of butts littered; 3B+ per year in the Bay Area). TerraCycle's own pages still do not mention gamma sterilization — the trade-secret framing stands, with the disclosure now on record from the partner side.
