## Relevance note
Program-era snapshot with the FIRST cigarette-specific quantities in the corpus: brigade totals at capture date = 6,172 collection locations, 328 open slots, 25,768,769 units collected. Confirms Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company sponsorship ($1.00 per pound donated to Keep America Beautiful's Cigarette Litter Prevention Program), the process line "recycled into a variety of industrial products, such as plastic pallets, and any remaining tobacco is recycled as compost", the accepted-waste list (extinguished cigarettes, filters, loose tobacco pouches, outer plastic packaging, inner foil packaging, rolling paper, ash; paperboard box packaging NOT accepted), and collection instructions (re-sealable plastic bags, fully extinguished, no paper bags). Links to a Cigarette Waste Brigade FAQ that is NOT in the capture and has not survived anywhere we can find.
