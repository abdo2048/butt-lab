CASE STUDY | KEEP NEW HANOVER BEAUTIFUL CIGARETTE 
RECYCLING PROGRAM
Keep New Hanover Beautiful and TerraCycle 
partner to collect and recycle cigarette butts 
across New Hanover County, including the 
Wilmington International Airport, New 
Hanover County Government facilities, the 
Town of Wrightsville Beach, and downtown 
businesses.
Keep New Hanover Beautiful engages student 
volunteers and interns to service the 
receptacles on a weekly basis. Through the 
program they have collected and recycled 
over 1.8 million cigarette butts.
Keep New Hanover Beautiful 
provides the community with 
program-branded collection 
receptacles and supplies. 
Keep New Hanover Beautiful created promotional 
videos to drive awareness around the program.
TerraCycle drives awareness around the recycling 
program through customized PR campaigns. 
Dick Brightman,
Executive Director

CASE STUDY | CIGARETTE BUTT RECYCLING AT AMAZON
FACILITIES THROUGH FREE TERRACYCLE PROGRAM
Amazon now offers cigarette butt recycling as 
a sustainability benefit to employees across 
its network of 278 fulfillment centers across 
the US and Canada.
With the aid of TerraCycle’s customized 
cigarette receptacles, Amazon FCs have 
recycled over 2,000,000 cigarette butts since 
Earth Day 2021 from just a small sample of 
sites, and are now rolling out the program to 
all 278 locations.

CASE STUDY | CHUMASH CASINO
FREE CIGARETTE RECYCLING PROGRAM
Chumash Casino Resort partners with TerraCycle to 
collect and recycle cigarette butts from its casino. 
Since 2013, Chumash has sent nearly 29 million 
cigarette butts to TerraCycle to be recycled into 
products such as plastic shipping pallets, parks 
benches and picnic tables.
The Custodial Services Management 
Team manages and collects the 
casino’s cigarette butts in a dedicated 
collection bin back of house. 
The free program is part of Chumash’s sustainability 
practices and helped the casino to become the first 
casino in the world to earn TRUE© (Total Resource 
Use and Efficiency) Zero Waste certification from 
Green Business Certification Inc. (GBCI), diverting 90 
percent of its waste stream from landfills.

CASE STUDY |DISNEY CRUISE LINE
 FREE CIGARETTE WASTE RECYCLING PROGRAM
Disney Cruise Line piloted participation in the free cigarette 
waste recycling program on their Port Canaveral-based 
Disney Fantasy ship beginning in March 2022. In just 10 
months the single vessel collected and recycled over 
740,000 cigarette butts through the program. Additionally, 
Disney Fantasy piloted razor recycling and other waste 
streams, collectively diverting over 1,000 pounds waste 
from landfill and incineration.
Following the success of Disney Fantasy the rest of 
Disney Cruise Lines North America fleet have joined 
both the cigarette and razor recycling programs.
Based on the success of the pilot, Disney Cruise 
Lines is expanding participation in the free cigarette 
waste recycling program and free razor recycling 
program to its entire North American fleet.
