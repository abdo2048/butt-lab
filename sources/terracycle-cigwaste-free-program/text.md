How it works
We’ve made getting started with the Cigarette Waste Free Recycling Program easy! Simply request a QR code or shipping label with FedEx, or download a shipping label via UPS to send your cigarette waste to TerraCycle, and we’ll recycle it.
Important note
: In order to recycle this waste stream properly, make sure the cigarettes are fully extinguished. Please do not dump your cigarette waste directly into the box you plan to ship them in. Additionally, do not use paper bags to collect your waste. Instead, collect cigarette waste using sturdy plastic containers or bags.
You can order exclusive receptacles designed specifically for recycling cigarette waste in your community or at your business location. Receptacles are perfect for standardizing cigarette recycling in small- or large-scale outdoor environments. These metal receptacles can be equipped with steel bolt bands for flexible placement at your location.

What can I send in through this recycling program?
Send in extinguished cigarettes, cigarette filters, loose tobacco pouches, outer plastic packaging, inner foil packaging, rolling paper, and ash
Please note:
We do not accept the paperboard packaging of a cigarette box. Instead, you may recycle this through your municipal recycling program.
Can I only collect Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company-brand products?
No, the Cigarette Waste Free Recycling Program accepts cigarette waste from all brands.
How do I reduce the odor of my collected waste?
It is important to pick an appropriate container to store your collected waste. To avoid odors, it would be best to collect your waste in an airtight container or by using an odor resistant/scented bag. Additionally, using odor absorbing substances such as baking soda or charcoal briquettes can help reduce odor if you put them in your waste container. When discarding your cigarettes, make sure you remove as much of the residual tobacco as possible, as the tobacco is the main source of odor. Lastly, make sure your waste is as dry as possible because moisture increases odor.
Are there any costs associated with participating in this program?
Santa Fe Natural Tobacco Company has sponsored this free recycling program, so there is no direct cost to you.
What are the best practices for shipping my recycling to TerraCycle?
We encourage you to ship when your box is full of cigarette waste to minimize the transportation carbon footprint for this program. Shipping carriers will not accept dripping packages, so please make sure your items are dry.
Your box shouldn’t be heavier than 50 lbs or larger than 18x18x18".
What are your recommended processes if I am collecting cigarette waste at home?
To collect and ship for the Cigarette Waste Free Recycling Program from home, simply empty the contents of your ash tray into a re-sealable plastic bag, disposable plastic container, plastic shopping bag, or garbage bag.
Please make sure that the cigarettes are fully extinguished.
Do not dump your cigarette waste directly into the shipping box and do not use paper bags or envelopes to collect your waste. Ship your collections to TerraCycle by downloading a free shipping label from your TerraCycle account. Ship the box to TerraCycle by affixing the free shipping label and dropping it off at a UPS location.
What are your recommended processes if I am collecting cigarette waste at an office or business establishment or for my community?
To collect and ship for the Cigarette Waste Free Recycling Program at your office or business establishment, empty the contents of your ash receptacle into a sturdy trash bag or thick garbage liner.
Please make sure that the cigarettes are fully extinguished.
Do not dump your cigarette waste directly into the shipping box and do not use paper bags to collect your waste. Ship your collections to TerraCycle by downloading a free shipping label from your TerraCycle account. Ship the box to TerraCycle by affixing the free shipping label and dropping it off at a UPS location.

No, you do not need to clean the packaging before dropping it off, but please remove as much of the remaining product as possible before shipping.
