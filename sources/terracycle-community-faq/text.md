I already have an account. Can I use it to recycle for my community?
You may use an existing account; just be sure to update your Organization Type (e.g., 4H Group, Retail Store, Other) and indicate how many people you will be collecting recycling for under # People Collecting.
Why are some free recycling programs so specific?
Our free recycling programs are funded by brands and retailers. They specify which type of waste is accepted by their programs, meaning that some programs only accept one brand’s waste while others accept all brands.
You can find our all-brand programs here
.
Is there a limit to how much trash I can send in?
You can send in as much as you’d like! The more you send in for recycling, the better for your community and the planet. Plus,
you’ll earn more TerraCycle points
.

Learn how to redeem your points, add a new charity to our database, and more here

What does TerraCycle do with my trash?
Items are cleaned and separated by material type and recycled into raw formats used to make new products.
