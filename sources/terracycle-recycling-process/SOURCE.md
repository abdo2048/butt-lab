## Relevance note
The current company-wide process description (not cigarette-specific): waste received at TerraCycle Material Recovery Facilities, scanned (date/weight/material), manually inspected for non-compliant waste, aggregated for processing partners; chain-of-custody tracking; sortation technologies enumerated (manual, size screens, sink/float, optical/NIR with air jets, air density, magnetic); organics composted or used in fertilizers; plastics "sorted by type, then melted and reformatted into pellets, flakes, or a powder format"; end products list (outdoor furniture and decking, plastic shipping pallets, watering cans, storage containers, construction tubes, flooring tiles, playground surfaces, athletic fields); waste-to-energy used only for non-compliant waste. Company claims: over 95% of collected waste processed in the region where collected; 98.3% of all materials received recycled.
