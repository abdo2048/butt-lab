# Batch-02 Launch Plan — Method Explanations, Gap-Closing, and Invention

**Status:** v1.0 — operator decisions recorded 2026-11-13 · **Predecessor:** batch-01 (closed, commit e8665f9)

## Operator decisions (2026-11-13)

1. **Scope:** all six method families, fully covered.
2. **Execution:** single AI (no panel). Method explanations with inline gap-closing.
3. **J-H-01:** prior-art awareness variant — AI sees previous methods and batch-01 outputs to know what is already there and avoid previous mistakes, not as a reference or starting point; must explicitly record what is not good about previous methods.
4. **Jurisdiction:** main content for a general worldwide audience, with a separate Egypt annex. Product focus: all options remain open; decided after Phase 2. A possible future AI review of batch-02 outputs is EXCLUDED from this workflow (operator decision).

## Purpose

Batch-02 extends batch-01 from a company-centered evidence review to a broader method-landscape review with readable explanations, inline gap-closing, and a fresh AI invention round — grounded in the enriched evidence base from batch-01's gap-closing unit.

## Scope — method families to cover (all six)

1. Mechanical/physical — washing, sorting, compounding
2. Thermal — pyrolysis, gasification, incineration-with-energy-recovery
3. Chemical — solvent dissolution, supercritical CO2, depolymerization
4. Biological — composting, anaerobic digestion, enzymatic
5. Material incorporation — cement/brick/asphalt, polymer composites
6. Product routes — cellulose pulp, activated carbon, adsorbents, insulation

## Phase 1 — Method explanations with inline gap-closing

For each method family, ONE readable explanation document:

- **Audience:** medicine-faculty students and interested general readers.
- **Source-grounded:** based on the original source files (patents, papers, company/regulatory documents in materials/gap-closing/ and packets/).
- **Inline gap-closing:** where the original source is missing information at a specific point, the AI inserts its own gap-closing explanation AT THAT EXACT LOCATION — never at the end or the beginning of the file — marked distinctly as:

  > **[GAP-CLOSING NOTE — AI, scientist-inventor role]** ...

  Source-derived content and AI gap-closing must remain visually and textually distinguishable at all times.
- Each explanation covers: mechanism, feedstock requirements, contaminant fate, energy/water use, product quality, regulatory status, cost signals, evidence quality.

## Phase 2 — Invention after seeing all methods

After all six explanations are complete, the AI develops its own overall proposal/invention:

- The AI reviews the previous methods AND batch-01 outputs (J-H-01, A/B/C proposals, judge findings) as **prior-art awareness only** — to know what is already there, avoid previous mistakes, and explicitly record what is not good about previous methods.
- The invention must state its own four-field sink assumptions (material, intended use, acceptance basis, issuer) and respect the metals stop-condition.
- The invention is a new unvalidated hypothesis, not an improvement note on J-H-01.

## Egypt annex

Main content is written for a general worldwide audience. A separate annex file covers Egypt-specific regulatory and practical considerations.

## Product focus

All product routes remain open. No product gate is chosen in this plan; the decision is deferred until after Phase 2.
