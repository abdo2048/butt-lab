# Batch-02 Launch Plan — Method Landscape, Deep-Dive, and Invention

**Status:** draft for operator approval · **Date:** 2026-11-12 · **Predecessor:** batch-01 (closed, commit e8665f9)

## Purpose

Batch-02 extends batch-01 from a company-centered evidence review to a broader method-landscape review, deeper per-method analysis, and a fresh AI invention round — now grounded in the enriched evidence base from batch-01's gap-closing unit.

## Scope — method families to cover

Expanded beyond batch-01's 10-company map:

1. Mechanical/physical — washing, sorting, compounding
2. Thermal — pyrolysis, gasification, incineration-with-energy-recovery
3. Chemical — solvent dissolution, supercritical CO2, depolymerization
4. Biological — composting, anaerobic digestion, enzymatic
5. Material incorporation — cement/brick/asphalt, polymer composites
6. Product routes — cellulose pulp, activated carbon, adsorbents, insulation

Each method family is assessed on: mechanism, feedstock requirements, contaminant fate, energy/water use, product quality, regulatory status, cost signals, evidence quality.

## Workflow structure (mirrors batch-01)

- Stage 1 — Harvester: method-landscape map (broader than company map; academic + patent + industrial routes)
- Stage 2 — Deep-dive: per-method reconstruction with the four-field sink framework
- Stage 3 — Red-team panel: adversarial analysis (3 slots + judge)
- Stage 4 — Inventor panel: AI invention informed by the full method landscape (new hypotheses, not just company-based)
- Stage 5 — Synthesis + validation/open questions

## Key differences from batch-01

- Broader method scope (academic + patent + industrial, not just 10 companies)
- Richer evidence base (gap-closing corpus now in butt-lab)
- Invention round explicitly informed by the full method landscape, not just company disclosures
- Same guardrails: no experiment authorized, no safety/novelty/deployment claims, metals stop-condition, four-field sink discipline

## Open design decisions for operator

- How many method families to cover (all six above, or a subset?)
- Reuse the same panel/judge structure or adjust?
- Does J-H-01 (batch-01's invention) carry forward as a baseline for comparison, or get retired?
- Jurisdiction/product focus for the R-gaps (Egypt/EU/US? recycled pulp? CA composite? compost?)

## Guardrails (unchanged from batch-01)

- No experiment authorized by batch-02 planning alone
- No claim that any method is clean, safe, validated, or environmentally superior without measurement
- Metals remain an explicit stop condition
- Four-field sink accounting (material, intended use, acceptance basis, issuer) required for any proposal
- Desk synthesis only; experimental validation requires separate operator authorization
