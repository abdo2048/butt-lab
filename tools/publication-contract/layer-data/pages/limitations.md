# Limitations, open questions, and where this project is weakest

This page collects everything the project does not know. The gaps are deliberately listed rather than summarised away, because the most useful thing a reader can take away is a clear view of them.

Nothing here was measured. Every gap below is open, and every one of them has been left open rather than filled with an estimate.

These are future measurement and decision work, not retrieval work. Searching harder cannot close them; only authorised measurement can.

## Eight measurement gaps

The complete comparable target-panel dataset does not exist for any route. These are the analyte-by-stream boundaries that would have to be closed, one per boundary.

| # | Boundary | What is missing there |
|---|---|---|
| M01 | Whole feed against the separated filter, tobacco, paper, ash and fines | Nicotine, each TSNA and PAH, and each metal mass by fraction on the same dry-feed basis. The controlling fraction remains unknown, so a partition ranking is required. |
| M02 | Staged-wash liquids and the recovered acetate | Target organics and metals in each water and ethanol fraction and in the product, plus a complete liquid inventory and the final treatment fate. |
| M03 | Initial wash, later wash, recycle, purge, sludge and acetate in the mechanical route | Per-analyte mass, cycle inventory trend, residual product content, any sludge-to-product contribution, treatment inlet and outlet, and air-control fate. |
| M04 | Fibre, composite, off-gas and condensate in the thermal route | Per-analyte retention against transfer, metal and species fate, captured emissions, and product leaching. Formulation dilution cannot supply removal data. |
| M05 | Precipitate, mother liquor, wash, insolubles and recovered solvent in the chemical route | Organics co-precipitation, cadmium, lead, arsenic and mercury phase transfer, solvent and acid residuals and recovery, capture residues, and final outlet performance. |
| M06 | Fibre, concentrate, recycle, and any sorbents or filters in the dense-fluid route | Actual phase and recipe-linked extraction per analyte, metal fate, concentrate composition, purge and loss, and final treatment fate. |
| M07 | Pulp, dark liquor, purge, precipitate and wash water in the pulping route | Individual toxicant masses rather than a bulk chemical-oxygen-demand figure alone, inventory growth, outlet load, precipitate composition, and pulp contact and leachate evidence. |
| M08 | Compost, lumber, other final articles, and end-of-life release | Product-specific analyte content, exposure-relevant leaching and emissions, ecotoxicity where it is required, and mechanical and functional evidence. |

A written measurement plan and a written feasibility sub-study specification already exist for the first of these, and both name their inputs instead of fixing a sample count. Two documents are not a measurement, and no sample count exists.

## Five jurisdiction and regulatory acceptance gaps

| # | Gap |
|---|---|
| R01 | **Selected jurisdiction and material status.** The governing feed and output waste classification, transport, storage and treatment permissions, and the basis for end-of-waste status for a selected plant and product. A feed classification established in one country cannot simply be exported to every other country or to every output. |
| R02 | **Actual final recipient.** For each liquid, solid and capture residual: the permitted recipient, the actual acceptance specification, treatment residuals, discharge basis and a demonstrated analytical outlet. Naming an external purification step answers none of these. |
| R03 | **Intended-use criteria.** The exact contact, leaching and migration, emissions, ecotoxicity and performance acceptance bases for the proposed acetate and cellulose articles, compost and composites. There is no universal product limit, and no automatic downgrade-to-restricted-use rule is adopted here. |
| R04 | **Standards and hazard-property extrapolation.** The hazard-property and compost-standard discussion rests on search excerpts and secondary material. Neither numerical standard limits nor the assertion that removing nicotine alone clears a legal hazard property is accepted without authoritative, material-specific verification. |
| R05 | **Equipment, exposure and intellectual-property review.** Solvent, chemical, pressure, air-emission and occupational obligations remain questions for qualified engineering and legal review. Patent-status shorthand and term arithmetic are not novelty, patentability or freedom-to-operate determinations. |

## Six scale, cost and energy gaps

| # | Missing denominator or inventory | Why the comparison stays open |
|---|---|---|
| S01 | Energy per mass of received wet butts, dry whole butts, dry filter feed and conforming output | Moisture removal, compression, solvent recovery, treatment and rejected product must all be included, on explicit and non-interchangeable bases. |
| S02 | Water, reagent and solvent makeup and purge, per dry feed and per conforming output | A recycling label does not define net consumption, inventory growth or final waste load. |
| S03 | Full-cycle throughput and utilisation | Dissolution duration is not whole-process residence time, and collection counts and nominal capacity do not define moisture-adjusted accepted feed or conforming throughput. |
| S04 | Treatment cost per stream volume, per dry residual mass and per analyte load | Dilute liquid, metals-bearing residues, captured emissions and product rejects may control disposition, and no accepted price or affordable route was located. |
| S05 | Product revenue, function and displacement | Recovered acetate, cellulose pulp and composite filler are different products, and a functional unit and a conforming output are needed before any cost or environmental comparison. |
| S06 | Complete system inventory and comparator | Collection, transport, energy source, additives, treatment, emissions, residual disposal, end-of-life and displaced function. The one company retreat in the record is not an audit-ready cross-family assessment. |

## What is unresolved rather than missing

Some items are not gaps in the literature. They are live disagreements inside the record, and they are recorded rather than settled.

- **The primary hypothesis remains an unvalidated judge hypothesis.** It is the preferred revision target and not an accepted process.
- **The minority position is preserved.** The route that recovers polymer of a particular quality may be the better starting point when polymer quality is the controlling objective. That position was never merged into the main line, and it survives.
- **Two secondary hypotheses sit behind the main one.** The earlier proposals they came from keep their provenance and are represented through the primary hypothesis's new combination.
- **Preserved dissent.** The objection about routing the wrong stream and diluting it into a purge, the objection about unsolved metals, the warning about dilution and off-gas in the thermal route, and the absence of comparable scale evidence all survive.

## The limits of the method itself

- The second desk cycle ran as a single artificial-intelligence execution with no three-model panel and no judge packet, so its documents are a disciplined single-perspective synthesis rather than an independently reviewed result.
- Retrieval was not re-performed during that cycle. The evidence basis is the accepted first-cycle record, the gap-closing evidence, the method registry and the source reconstructions. Any new primary source found later is an improvement and should be recorded as one, not folded in silently.
- A provenance follow-up about reconciling recorded hashes against raw committed bytes remains open. It is an operator-owned record-keeping question, and it is not permission to reopen a scientific input or to alter a chemistry verdict.
- No independent, public, full life-cycle assessment of a commercial butt-recycling process was located, so the site has no comparator to measure the families against.

## What would change these limitations

- **Authorised, resourced measurement** in a qualified facility, with a risk assessment and lawful handling of contaminated feed, under a written protocol and an analyst who can be held to it.
- **A chosen jurisdiction and a chosen product**, so that a single set of acceptance criteria can be named and then tested against.
- **A named final recipient for every residual stream**, including the dilute liquids and the metals-bearing residues, with a demonstrated analytical outlet.
- **An independent review of the desk cycle** by someone other than the system that produced it.

Until those exist, the correct summary of this project is that it has mapped the methods and located the gaps precisely, and that nothing has been demonstrated.
